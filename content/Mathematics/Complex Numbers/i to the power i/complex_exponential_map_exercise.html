The exponential map: aim the output

Set a complex input z = a + b·i and watch where w = exp(r·z) lands. The real part a controls how far out; the imaginary part b controls how far around.

Pick a target above.
What you're seeing. The dashed circle is the image of the imaginary axis (every pure-imaginary input lands on the unit circle). The orange ray with log-spaced ticks is the image of the real axis (real inputs land on er·k — powers of 2 when r = ln 2). The faint orange circle is the locus of all outputs at the current a, as b sweeps.
