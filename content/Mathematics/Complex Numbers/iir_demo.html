Try this: set T = π, then drag n from 2 to 400. Notice the endpoint sliding inward toward exactly −1 on the real axis. The "gap" value shows how far off the discrete answer is from the continuous one.
The discrete process: each step multiplies by (1 + i·Δt), which rotates by arctan(Δt) ≈ Δt radians and scales by √(1 + Δt²) > 1. The scaling is what causes the outward spiral. As Δt → 0, both the rotation per step and the excess scaling shrink in lock-step, and the path collapses onto pure rotation along the unit circle.
