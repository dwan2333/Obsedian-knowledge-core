The Tower Machine  f(x) = (√2)x

Each press of Step feeds the latest number back into the machine: xn+1 = f(xn). The latest step is drawn bright with arrows and labels; zoom in to watch the endgame up close.

Press Step. Each step is two moves: ① up/down to the curve (the machine computes f(xn)) then ② across to the mirror line (the output becomes the next input).
nxngap εn = xn−2gap ratio
Fixed points sit where the curve crosses the mirror line: y = 2 (slope ln 2 ≈ 0.69 < 1 — each step multiplies the gap by ~0.69, staircase pulled in) and y = 4 (slope ln 4 ≈ 1.39 > 1 — gaps grow, staircase pushed away). Zoom to 8× or 20× with “follow the action” on: near 2 the blue curve and orange tangent become indistinguishable — the visual meaning of xn+1 ≈ y + ln(y)(xn − y).
