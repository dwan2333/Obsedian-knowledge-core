# Critiquing & Dismissing

*Scenario category — words for putting something down: judging an effort, idea, or thing as inadequate, dull, or absurd.*

When you want to register disapproval with precision — too plain, too small, or too ridiculous to respect.

## Words
- [prosaic](<../../Words/prosaic.md>) — dismissed as dull and commonplace
- [ludicrous](<../../Words/ludicrous.md>) — dismissed as laughably absurd
- [puny](<../../Words/puny.md>) — dismissed as feebly inadequate
- [frivolous](<../../Words/frivolous.md>) — not serious; trivial and silly
- [obscure](<../../Words/obscure.md>) — unclear or little-known
- [snigger](<../../Words/snigger.md>) — a sly, mocking laugh
- [ridicule](<../../Words/ridicule.md>) — mockery meant to belittle
- [revolting](<../../Words/revolting.md>) — disgusting; repulsive
