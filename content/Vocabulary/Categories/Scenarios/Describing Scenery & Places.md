# Describing Scenery & Places

*Scenario category — words for evoking a landscape or setting: how a place looks and feels to stand in.*

When you're painting a scene — the charm of a view or the drama of its terrain.

## Words
- [picturesque](<../../Words/picturesque.md>) — charmingly scenic, like a picture
- [precipice](<../../Words/precipice.md>) — a sheer, steep cliff face
- [monastery](<../../Words/monastery.md>) — a community and house of monks
- [oleander](<../../Words/oleander.md>) — a flowering (poisonous) shrub
