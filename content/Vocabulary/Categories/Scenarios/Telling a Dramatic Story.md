# Telling a Dramatic Story

*Scenario category — words that carry narrative drama: sudden turns, secret flights, and moments on the brink.*

When you're spinning a tale and need the language of suspense, impulse, and high stakes.

## Words
- [elopement](<../../Words/elopement.md>) — a secret flight to marry
- [caprice](<../../Words/caprice.md>) — a sudden, story-turning whim
- [precipice](<../../Words/precipice.md>) — the brink of disaster
- [debauchery](<../../Words/debauchery.md>) — excessive indulgence in sensual pleasure
- [indignation](<../../Words/indignation.md>) — anger at unfairness or wrong
- [trample](<../../Words/trample.md>) — to tread underfoot; to crush or oppress
