# Describing a Person

*Scenario category — words to reach for when you're characterising someone: their temperament, their bearing, their mind, or their looks.*

When you want to pin down what a person is *like* — sharp or servile, strong or feeble, mysterious or easily led.

- [abject](<../../Words/abject.md>) — cringing and servile, without dignity
- [toady](<../../Words/toady.md>) — a fawning flatterer
- [shrewd](<../../Words/shrewd.md>) — sharp and astute in judgment
- [vigorous](<../../Words/vigorous.md>) — strong and full of energy
- [puny](<../../Words/puny.md>) — small and weak
- [enigmatic](<../../Words/enigmatic.md>) — mysterious and hard to read
- [pliable](<../../Words/pliable.md>) — easily influenced or led
- [voluptuous](<../../Words/voluptuous.md>) — full-figured; sensually alluring
- [dissipated](<../../Words/dissipated.md>) — dissolute; given to debauchery
- [profligate](<../../Words/profligate.md>) — recklessly wasteful and dissolute
- [malice](<../../Words/malice.md>) — ill will; the desire to harm
- [deacon](<../../Words/deacon.md>) — a junior ordained minister
- [unruly](<../../Words/unruly.md>) — disorderly; resistant to control
- [meekness](<../../Words/meekness.md>) — gentle submissiveness; humility
- [obstinate](<../../Words/obstinate.md>) — stubborn; refusing to yield
- [coarse](<../../Words/coarse.md>) — rough; crude or vulgar in manner
- [scrupulous](<../../Words/scrupulous.md>) — diligent and morally careful
- [morose](<../../Words/morose.md>) — gloomy and ill-humoured
- [sagacious](<../../Words/sagacious.md>) — wise and discerning
- [ardent](<../../Words/ardent.md>) — burning with passion or devotion
