# Medicine & Science

*Domain category — words at home in clinics, hospitals, health reporting, and scientific writing.*

The register of doctors, researchers, and health journalism — for naming conditions, treatments, and how well they work.

## Words
- [efficacy](<../../Words/efficacy.md>) — the power to produce the intended result; how well a treatment works
- [acute](<../../Words/acute.md>) — sharp and intense, as of pain or a crisis
- [debilitating](<../../Words/debilitating.md>) — weakening; sapping strength
- [gurney](<../../Words/gurney.md>) — a wheeled hospital stretcher
- [malpractice](<../../Words/malpractice.md>) — negligent conduct by a professional, especially a doctor
- [perspiration](<../../Words/perspiration.md>) — sweat
- [sedentary](<../../Words/sedentary.md>) — involving much sitting and little activity
- [relapse](<../../Words/relapse.md>) — to fall ill again after improving
