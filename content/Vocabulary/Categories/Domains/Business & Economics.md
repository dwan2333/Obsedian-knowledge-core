# Business & Economics

*Domain category — words at home in business news, company reports, economics, and public budgets.*

The working vocabulary of money in motion: how firms, markets, and governments spend, cut back, and respond to incentives.

## Words
- [retrench](<../../Words/retrench.md>) — to cut back spending; to scale down
- [disincentive](<../../Words/disincentive.md>) — something that discourages an action, especially an economic one
- [inducement](<../../Words/inducement.md>) — an incentive to act
- [concession](<../../Words/concession.md>) — a price reduction or business right; ground given in a deal
- [moat](<../../Words/moat.md>) — a lasting competitive advantage
- [espionage](<../../Words/espionage.md>) — (industrial espionage) stealing trade secrets
