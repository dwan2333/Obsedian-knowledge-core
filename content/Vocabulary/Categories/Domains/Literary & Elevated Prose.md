# Literary & Elevated Prose

*Domain category — words that belong to formal, literary, and elevated writing: essays, fiction, and criticism rather than casual speech.*

The heightened register — words that lend writing dignity, colour, or nuance, and that would sound ornate in ordinary conversation.

## Words
- [abject](<../../Words/abject.md>) — utterly wretched; servile
- [enigmatic](<../../Words/enigmatic.md>) — mysterious and hard to interpret
- [insuperable](<../../Words/insuperable.md>) — impossible to overcome
- [caprice](<../../Words/caprice.md>) — an impulsive whim
- [voluptuous](<../../Words/voluptuous.md>) — richly sensual
- [picturesque](<../../Words/picturesque.md>) — charmingly scenic
- [prosaic](<../../Words/prosaic.md>) — dull and commonplace
- [piquancy](<../../Words/piquancy.md>) — a pleasantly sharp quality
- [importunity](<../../Words/importunity.md>) — persistent, pressing demands
