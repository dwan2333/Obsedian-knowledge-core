# Literary & Elevated Prose

*Domain category — words that belong to formal, literary, and elevated writing: essays, fiction, and criticism rather than casual speech.*

The heightened register — words that lend writing dignity, colour, or nuance, and that would sound ornate in ordinary conversation.
