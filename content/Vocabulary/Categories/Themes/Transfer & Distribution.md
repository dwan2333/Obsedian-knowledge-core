# Transfer & Distribution

*Theme category — words about moving things and dividing them up: carrying, transferring, and allotting in shares.*

How goods, property, and responsibility are passed along or parcelled out.

## Words
- [apportion](<../../Words/apportion.md>) — divide and allot in fitting shares
- [conveyance](<../../Words/conveyance.md>) — a vehicle; the act of carrying; the legal transfer of property
- [installment](<../../Words/installment.md>) — a part-payment in a series
- [parcel](<../../Words/parcel.md>) — a package; a plot of land; (parcel out) to divide and hand out
