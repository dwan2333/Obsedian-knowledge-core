# Obstacles & Danger

*Theme category — words about what blocks the way or threatens disaster: barriers that can't be passed and edges that shouldn't be crossed.*

Things that stand in the way or loom as a hazard — from the impassable to the precipitous.

## Words
- [insuperable](<../../Words/insuperable.md>) — (of an obstacle) impossible to overcome
- [precipice](<../../Words/precipice.md>) — a sheer cliff; the brink of disaster
- [scrape](<../../Words/scrape.md>) — an awkward predicament; a tight spot
