# Permanence & Endurance

*Theme category — words for what lasts and cannot be undone: the everlasting, the unbreakable bond, the habit fixed too deep to shift.*

The vocabulary of permanence — things that endure without end, resist all breaking, or are rooted past removal.

## Words
- [perpetual](<../../Words/perpetual.md>) — never ending; continuous
- [indissoluble](<../../Words/indissoluble.md>) — unable to be broken or undone
- [ingrained](<../../Words/ingrained.md>) — fixed so deep it cannot be shifted
