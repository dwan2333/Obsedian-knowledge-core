# Power, Freedom & Servitude

*Theme category — words about domination and submission: holding power, suffering under it, bowing to it, and breaking free of it.*

Vocabulary for the whole arc of power over people — from the tyrant who wields it, through those who grovel before it, to the liberation that ends it.

## Words
- [abject](<../../Words/abject.md>) — wretched; cringingly servile and stripped of dignity
- [toady](<../../Words/toady.md>) — a servile flatterer who fawns over the powerful
- [despotism](<../../Words/despotism.md>) — cruel, absolute rule; tyranny
- [emancipation](<../../Words/emancipation.md>) — liberation; a setting-free from control
