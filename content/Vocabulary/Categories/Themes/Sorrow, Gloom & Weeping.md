# Sorrow, Gloom & Weeping

*Theme category — words for grief and low spirits: gloom, sulkiness, the sharp stab of pain, and the sounds of weeping.*

The vocabulary of sadness in all its shades — from a brooding, sullen gloom to inconsolable grief and the tears it brings.

## Words
- [morose](<../../Words/morose.md>) — gloomy and ill-humoured
- [sullenness](<../../Words/sullenness.md>) — silent, sulky bad temper
- [inconsolable](<../../Words/inconsolable.md>) — grief-stricken beyond comfort
- [pang](<../../Words/pang.md>) — a sudden sharp stab of feeling
- [moan](<../../Words/moan.md>) — a low sound of pain or grief
- [blubber](<../../Words/blubber.md>) — to weep noisily and messily
