# Speech, Frankness & the Unspoken

*Theme category — words for how we express ourselves: stating a view, speaking frankly, and what is left implied rather than said.*

The vocabulary of expression — from the opinion declared and the truth spoken plainly to the meaning carried unspoken beneath the words.

## Words
- [opine](<../../Words/opine.md>) — to state as one's opinion
- [candid](<../../Words/candid.md>) — frank and openly honest
- [implicit](<../../Words/implicit.md>) — implied though not directly stated
- [digress](<../../Words/digress.md>) — to wander off the subject
- [utterance](<../../Words/utterance.md>) — something spoken
- [ascribe](<../../Words/ascribe.md>) — to attribute to a source
- [parlance](<../../Words/parlance.md>) — a group's particular way of speaking
- [reticence](<../../Words/reticence.md>) — reluctance to reveal one's thoughts or feelings
