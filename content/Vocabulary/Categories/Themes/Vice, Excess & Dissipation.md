# Vice, Excess & Dissipation

*Theme category — words for dissolute living, indulgence, and waste.*

Vocabulary for excess and dissipation: the dissolute life and the squandering of time, money, or virtue.

- [debauchery](<../../Words/debauchery.md>) — excessive indulgence in sensual pleasure
- [dissipated](<../../Words/dissipated.md>) — dissolute; given over to debauchery
- [profligate](<../../Words/profligate.md>) — recklessly wasteful and dissolute
