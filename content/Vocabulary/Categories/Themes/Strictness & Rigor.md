# Strictness & Rigor

*Theme category — words about strictness: rules and standards that demand much and allow little, and the severe manner that enforces them.*

The vocabulary of firm standards — how tight a rule is, how exacting a test, how unbending the person who applies it.

## Words
- [stringent](<../../Words/stringent.md>) — (of rules and standards) strict and demanding
- [stern](<../../Words/stern.md>) — serious, strict, and unrelenting in manner
- [clamp](<../../Words/clamp.md>) — (clamp down on) to act firmly to stop or limit something
