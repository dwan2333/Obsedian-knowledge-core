# Strength & Weakness

*Theme category — words about physical and figurative power: being strong, energetic, and forceful, or small, frail, and feeble.*

The spectrum from robust force to feeble inadequacy — of bodies, efforts, and things.

## Words
- [vigorous](<../../Words/vigorous.md>) — strong, energetic, forceful
- [puny](<../../Words/puny.md>) — small, weak, and insignificant
- [ironclad](<../../Words/ironclad.md>) — unbreakably firm; impossible to alter or escape
- [debilitating](<../../Words/debilitating.md>) — weakening; sapping strength
- [grit](<../../Words/grit.md>) — courage and dogged resolve
- [untenable](<../../Words/untenable.md>) — impossible to defend or keep up
- [dilapidated](<../../Words/dilapidated.md>) — fallen into disrepair through age and neglect
