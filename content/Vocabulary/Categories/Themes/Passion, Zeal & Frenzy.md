*Theme category — words for intense feeling: burning devotion, uncritical zeal, and the frantic states they can boil over into.*

The spectrum of fervour — from noble ardour, through unbalanced fanaticism, to wild, uncontrolled frenzy.

- [fanatic](<../../Words/fanatic.md>) — filled with excessive, uncritical zeal
