# Cleverness & Cunning

*Theme category — words about sharp practical intelligence: the wit to judge shrewdly and to find a way around any obstacle.*

Worldly cleverness — the mind that sizes things up quickly and outmanoeuvres what stands in its way.

## Words
- [shrewd](<../../Words/shrewd.md>) — sharp and astute in practical judgment
- [circumvent](<../../Words/circumvent.md>) — to cleverly get around an obstacle or rule
