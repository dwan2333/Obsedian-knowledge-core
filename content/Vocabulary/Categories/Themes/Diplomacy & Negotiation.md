# Diplomacy & Negotiation

*Theme category — words about settling differences by talking: representatives, missions, bargaining, and agreements.*

Vocabulary for the work of reaching agreement between sides, whether nations at a summit or relatives at a dinner table.

## Words
- [envoy](<../../Words/envoy.md>) — a representative sent on a diplomatic mission
- [amicably](<../../Words/amicably.md>) — in a friendly way, especially in settling a dispute
- [concession](<../../Words/concession.md>) — something given up to reach an agreement
- [espionage](<../../Words/espionage.md>) — spying between states; the hidden side of diplomacy
- [cede](<../../Words/cede.md>) — to hand over territory or control, often by treaty
- [bipartisan](<../../Words/bipartisan.md>) — backed by both of two opposing parties
