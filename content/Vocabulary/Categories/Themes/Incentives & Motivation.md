# Incentives & Motivation

*Theme category — words about what moves people to act or holds them back: rewards, deterrents, spurs, and motives.*

The vocabulary of why people do things: the carrots and sticks of economics, policy, and everyday persuasion.

## Words
- [inducement](<../../Words/inducement.md>) — something that persuades someone to act; an incentive
- [disincentive](<../../Words/disincentive.md>) — something that discourages an action
- [prod](<../../Words/prod.md>) — to poke; to push someone into acting
