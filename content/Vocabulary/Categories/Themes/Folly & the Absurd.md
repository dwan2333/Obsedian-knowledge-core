# Folly & the Absurd

*Theme category — words about the ridiculous: ideas and sights so unreasonable they invite laughter.*

The vocabulary of absurdity — what is too foolish or far-fetched to take seriously.

- [ludicrous](<../../Words/ludicrous.md>) — so absurd as to be laughable
- [frivolous](<../../Words/frivolous.md>) — not serious; trivial and silly
- [snigger](<../../Words/snigger.md>) — a sly, mocking, half-suppressed laugh
