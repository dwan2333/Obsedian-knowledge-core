# Ill Will, Anger & Resentment

*Theme category — words for hostility, grievance, and moral anger.*

Vocabulary for the darker social feelings: spite, resentment, and righteous indignation.

## Words
- [grievance](<../../Words/grievance.md>) — a real or imagined wrong; a complaint nursed
- [malice](<../../Words/malice.md>) — the active desire to harm; ill will
- [indignation](<../../Words/indignation.md>) — anger aroused by unfairness or wrong
- [spiteful](<../../Words/spiteful.md>) — moved by petty, malicious ill will
- [bitter](<../../Words/bitter.md>) — resentful; sharp and acrid
- [cynicism](<../../Words/cynicism.md>) — sneering distrust of others' motives
- [seething](<../../Words/seething.md>) — boiling with barely-contained anger
