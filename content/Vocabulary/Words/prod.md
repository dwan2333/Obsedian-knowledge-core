# prod

> [!definition] /prɒd/ (UK), /prɑːd/ (US) · *verb* · *noun*
> *Verb:* to poke someone or something with a finger, stick, or other pointed object. Figuratively, to urge or push someone, often someone reluctant, into doing something (*prod someone into acting*). *Noun:* a poke, or a reminder that pushes someone to act; also a pointed tool for driving animals (*a cattle prod*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He **prodded** the fire with a stick until it flared up.
> - It took a few **prods** from her manager before she finally sent the report.
> - The bad review **prodded** the restaurant into changing its menu.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal to neutral. The figurative sense usually implies the person needed pushing; *a gentle prod* is a polite reminder. Lighter than *pressure*, and less nagging than *pester*.
