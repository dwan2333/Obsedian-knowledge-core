# appealing

> [!definition] /əˈpiːlɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Attractive or pleasing; inviting interest or liking. (Also, of a look or gesture, imploring — *an appealing glance*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The idea of a quiet weekend was suddenly very **appealing**.
> - She has an **appealing**, open manner that puts strangers at ease.
> - He gave an **appealing** look, as if begging to be let off.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and positive — broad and friendly, covering anything from a tempting offer to a likeable personality. Note the second, imploring sense (an appealing look = a pleading one), which context makes clear.
