# pang

> [!definition] /pæŋ/ · *noun*
> A sudden sharp pain or spasm — of the body (*a pang of hunger*) or, more often, of feeling (*a pang of guilt, of longing*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **pang** of guilt struck him as he pocketed the change he hadn't earned.
> - She felt a sudden **pang** of homesickness at the smell of the bread.
> - The first **pangs** of hunger came around noon and grew steadily worse.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and widely usable, slightly literary in the emotional sense. A *pang* is brief and stabbing — it names the sudden inward twinge, not a settled sorrow. Pairs naturally with guilt, regret, longing, and hunger.
