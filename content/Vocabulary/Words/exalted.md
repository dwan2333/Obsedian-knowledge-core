# exalted

> [!definition] /ɪɡˈzɔːltɪd/ · *adjective*
> Raised to a high rank, dignity, or level; glorified. Also of a mood, elated and rapturous.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - From his **exalted** position on the board, he rarely met an ordinary employee.
> - The poem praises love in the most **exalted** terms.
> - She came out of the concert in an almost **exalted** state.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, positive but sometimes faintly ironic when the loftiness is overdone. It marks something as lifted high — in status, in language, or in feeling. Pairs with rank, ideals, and elevated emotion.
