# depravity

> [!definition] /dɪˈprævɪti/ · *noun*
> Moral corruption; wickedness, especially of a deep or habitual kind.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The trial laid bare a **depravity** no one had suspected behind his respectable manner.
> - He wrote about the city's underworld with a fascination bordering on relish for its **depravity**.
> - To her, the gambling den was a sink of **depravity** she forbade her sons to enter.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and severe — among the strongest words for moral rottenness. Heavier than *wickedness*; it implies corruption settled into the very character. Use sparingly, for genuine moral horror.
