# exhortation

> [!definition] /ˌɛɡzɔːˈteɪʃən/ · *noun*
> An address or urgent appeal strongly encouraging someone to do something.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The captain's half-time **exhortation** sent the team back out transformed.
> - For all his **exhortations** to thrift, the household spent as freely as ever.
> - The sermon was one long **exhortation** to charity.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, neutral-to-positive — it names rousing, hortatory speech meant to spur action, the language of sermons, speeches, and rallying cries. Distinct from an [admonition](admonition.md), which warns rather than urges onward.
