# sullenness

> [!definition] /ˈsʌlənnəs/ · *noun*
> Silent, sulky bad temper; a gloomy, resentful refusal to be pleased or to respond.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The boy's **sullenness** at dinner told everyone the punishment had landed.
> - She met every kindness with the same flat **sullenness**, as if warmth were a trap.
> - A heavy **sullenness** hung over the defeated camp.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative. *Sullenness* is gloom turned inward and slightly hostile — not loud anger but a brooding, uncooperative silence. It shades toward resentment more than pure sorrow.
