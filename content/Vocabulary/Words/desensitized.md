# desensitized

> [!definition] /diːˈsɛnsɪtaɪzd/ · *adjective*
> Made less sensitive or responsive; numbed to something through repeated exposure, so that it no longer affects one as it should. (Also spelled *desensitised*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Constant coverage had left viewers **desensitized** to the violence on screen.
> - Years on the ward had **desensitized** her to sights that horrify most people.
> - We risk becoming **desensitized** to bad news simply because there is so much of it.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative — it warns of an emotional or moral numbing, the dulling of a once-natural response. Common in writing about media, trauma, and conscience. The opposite is *sensitized* or *moved*.
