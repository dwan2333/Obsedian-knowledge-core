# unruly

> [!definition] /ʌnˈruːli/ · *adjective*
> Hard to control, discipline, or keep in order; disorderly and resistant to authority. Also of things — *unruly hair*, *an unruly mob* — that will not stay neat or contained.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The supply teacher could do nothing with the **unruly** class.
> - A few **unruly** strands of hair kept falling across her face.
> - By midnight the crowd had grown loud and **unruly**, and the stewards moved in.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral-to-mildly-disapproving. Plainer and more general than *insubordinate* or *recalcitrant*; reach for it when the resistance is messy and undirected rather than a pointed defiance.
