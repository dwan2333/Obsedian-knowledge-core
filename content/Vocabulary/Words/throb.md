# throb

> [!definition] /θrɒb/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To beat or pulsate strongly and rhythmically — of the heart, a wound, or an ache. As a noun, such a pulsation. (You met it as **throbbed**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - His head **throbbed** with every step on the way home.
> - Her heart **throbbed** as the name was called.
> - A low **throb** of bass came through the wall all night.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and vivid, neutral — it captures insistent, rhythmic pulsing, whether of pain, emotion, or sound. Pairs with heads, wounds, hearts, and engines; carries a bodily, felt quality.
