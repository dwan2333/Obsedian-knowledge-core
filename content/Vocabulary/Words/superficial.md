# superficial

> [!definition] /ˌsuːpəˈfɪʃəl/ · *adjective*
> On or near the surface only; lacking depth — of a wound, a resemblance, an understanding, or a person.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The cuts were **superficial** and healed within days.
> - His knowledge of the subject is broad but **superficial**.
> - It was a **superficial** friendship, all small talk and no substance.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and usually negative when applied to thought or character — it dismisses something as shallow, skating the surface without real depth. Neutral in the literal sense (a superficial wound). The opposite of *deep* or *profound*.
