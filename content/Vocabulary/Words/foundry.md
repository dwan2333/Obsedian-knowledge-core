# foundry

> [!definition] /ˈfaʊndri/ · *noun*
> A workshop or factory where metal is melted and cast into shapes.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **foundry** glowed all night, and the river ran warm below it.
> - He had worked the **foundry** since boyhood, and the heat had aged him early.
> - Church bells from that **foundry** still ring in a dozen towns.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete — the precise word for a metal-casting works. Evokes the industrial nineteenth century: heat, labour, and the making of everything from cannon to bells. No plainer synonym carries the same specific sense.
