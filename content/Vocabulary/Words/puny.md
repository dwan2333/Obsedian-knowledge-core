# puny

> [!definition] /ˈpjuːni/ · *adjective*
> Small and weak; undersized. By extension, feeble or pitifully inadequate — *a puny effort, a puny sum*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - His **puny** arms could barely lift the suitcase onto the rack.
> - Against the storm, the little harbour wall looked **puny** and temporary.
> - They offered a **puny** raise that didn't even cover the rise in rent.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and dismissive. *Puny* doesn't just measure size — it belittles, implying the thing is too small to matter or to succeed. The opposite of [vigorous](vigorous.md) and of anything robust.
