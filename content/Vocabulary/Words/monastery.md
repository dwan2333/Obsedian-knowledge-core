# monastery

> [!definition] /ˈmɒnəstri/ · *noun*
> A building or community where monks live apart from the world under religious vows, devoting themselves to prayer, work, and contemplation.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **monastery** clung to the cliffside, its bells carrying for miles across the valley.
> - He spent a year in a Tibetan **monastery**, rising before dawn to chant with the monks.
> - Within the **monastery** walls the only sounds were footsteps and the turning of pages.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete; at home in historical, religious, and travel writing. A *monastery* houses monks specifically — a *convent* houses nuns, and an *abbey* or *priory* names it by its rank. Evokes seclusion, austerity, and quiet.
