# debilitating

> [!definition] /dɪˈbɪlɪteɪtɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Making someone or something weak; sapping strength, energy, or function.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The illness was **debilitating**, leaving her bedridden for months.
> - A **debilitating** fear of failure kept him from ever starting.
> - Years of drought had a **debilitating** effect on the region's farms.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative — it names a serious, ongoing weakening, of the body, the will, or a system. Heavier than "tiring"; reach for it when something genuinely undermines capacity. The opposite of strengthening or [vigorous](vigorous.md).
