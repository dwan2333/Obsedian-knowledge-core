# enticing

> [!definition] /ɪnˈtaɪsɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Attractive in a way that tempts or lures; appealing enough to draw one in.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The smell of fresh bread drifting from the bakery was **enticing**.
> - They dangled an **enticing** offer: double the salary and a house thrown in.
> - There was something **enticing** about the unmarked door at the end of the hall.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and positive, with a built-in pull — to be *enticing* is not just to please but to *tempt*, to invite one closer. A shade more active and seductive than [appealing](appealing.md); pairs with offers, aromas, and temptations.
