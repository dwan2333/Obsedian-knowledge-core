# rudimentary

> [!definition] /ˌruːdɪˈmɛntəri/ · *adjective*
> Involving only the most basic principles; undeveloped, crude, or at an early stage.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He had only a **rudimentary** grasp of the language — enough to order coffee.
> - The shelter was **rudimentary**: four poles and a sheet of canvas.
> - In the embryo, the limbs are still **rudimentary**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-mildly-negative — it marks something as elementary, basic, or not fully developed, sometimes adequately so, sometimes inadequately. Cooler and more technical than "crude"; common in describing skills, tools, and early forms.
