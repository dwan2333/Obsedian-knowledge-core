# enigmatic

> [!definition] /ˌɛnɪɡˈmætɪk/ · *adjective*
> Mysterious and hard to interpret; puzzling in a way that invites curiosity. Often used of people whose thoughts or motives can't be read.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She gave an **enigmatic** smile and refused to say whether she'd accepted the offer.
> - The poem's **enigmatic** final line has been argued over for a century.
> - He remained an **enigmatic** figure at the firm — brilliant, private, and impossible to place.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and faintly literary, with a neutral-to-intriguing feel. Unlike *confusing*, which is a complaint, *enigmatic* is almost a compliment — it suggests a mystery worth dwelling on rather than a failure to be clear.
