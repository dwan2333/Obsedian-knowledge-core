# prosaic

> [!definition] /prəˈzeɪɪk/ · *adjective*
> Commonplace, dull, and unimaginative; lacking poetry or interest. Literally, having the plainness of prose rather than the elevation of verse.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The truth was disappointingly **prosaic**: the "ghost" was a loose shutter in the wind.
> - After the soaring overture, the second act felt flat and **prosaic**.
> - She had a **prosaic** way of describing even her most extraordinary adventures.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and mildly negative — a word of literary criticism that has spread into general use. It dismisses something as ordinary to the point of dullness. The opposite of *poetic* and of [picturesque](picturesque.md).
