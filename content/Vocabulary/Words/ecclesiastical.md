# ecclesiastical

> [!definition] /ɪˌkliːziˈæstɪkəl/ · *adjective*
> Relating to the Christian Church, its clergy, or its institutions.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The dispute was settled not in a civil court but by an **ecclesiastical** tribunal.
> - He wore the plain black of **ecclesiastical** office, with no ornament but a small cross.
> - The cathedral's **ecclesiastical** calendar dictated the rhythm of the whole town's year.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, the precise adjective for Church matters. At home in history, law, and religious writing; in everyday speech "church" (as in "church courts") is the plain equivalent.
