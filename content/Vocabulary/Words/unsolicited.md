# unsolicited

> [!definition] /ˌʌnsəˈlɪsɪtɪd/ · *adjective*
> Not asked for; given or sent without being requested.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He's always full of **unsolicited** advice about how to raise the children.
> - The agency does not accept **unsolicited** manuscripts.
> - An **unsolicited** offer arrived for the house before it even went on the market.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-faintly-negative and slightly formal — it flags something as uninvited, often with a hint that it was unwelcome (unsolicited advice, email, opinions). Common in business and legal contexts. The opposite is *solicited* or *requested*.
