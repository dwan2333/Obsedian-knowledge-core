# chaste

> [!definition] /tʃeɪst/ · *adjective*
> Abstaining from sex, especially that regarded as immoral; pure and virtuous. By extension, of style or decoration, simple and restrained.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She had taken a vow to remain **chaste**, and the convent's rule left no room to break it.
> - His prose is **chaste** — spare, unadorned, every ornament stripped away.
> - The painting's **chaste** colours, all greys and pale blues, suited the solemn subject.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and approving, with a moral or aesthetic weight. The direct opposite of [debauchery](debauchery.md) and of anything lush; reach for it when "pure" needs the added sense of *deliberate restraint*.
