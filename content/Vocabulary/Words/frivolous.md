# frivolous

> [!definition] /ˈfrɪvələs/ · *adjective*
> Not serious or sensible; concerned with trivial things in a way that is unsuitable to the occasion. Of a thing (a lawsuit, a complaint, an expense): so lacking in substance as not to deserve serious attention.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The judge threw out the **frivolous** lawsuit before it ever reached trial.
> - I can't justify such a **frivolous** purchase when the rent is due next week.
> - He was dismissed as a **frivolous** young man with no thought for tomorrow.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> General-register and negative, with a note of disapproval — it accuses someone or something of being lightweight where weight was called for. Milder than [ludicrous](ludicrous.md): frivolous is unserious, not absurd.
