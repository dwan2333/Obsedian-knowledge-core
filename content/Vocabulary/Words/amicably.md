# amicably

> [!definition] /ˈæmɪkəbli/ · *adverb*
> In a friendly, good-natured way; without hostility, especially in settling a disagreement.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The couple separated **amicably** and stayed on good terms.
> - The two sides finally settled the dispute **amicably**.
> - They parted **amicably**, with a handshake and no hard feelings.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-positive and slightly formal — most at home where friendliness is notable *because* conflict was possible (divorces, lawsuits, negotiations). The adjective is *amicable*; warmer than "peacefully," more cordial than "civilly."
