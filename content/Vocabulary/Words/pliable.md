# pliable

> [!definition] /ˈplaɪəbəl/ · *adjective*
> Easily bent without breaking; flexible. Of a person, easily influenced, persuaded, or controlled — *a pliable young recruit*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Soak the cane until it is **pliable** enough to weave into the frame.
> - The committee wanted a **pliable** chairman who would not question their decisions.
> - Children's habits are still **pliable**, which is why early routines matter so much.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral for materials; for people it tilts mildly negative, hinting that they are too easily led. *Flexible* praises adaptability; *pliable* often implies a will that yields too readily — to a whim ([caprice](caprice.md)) or to pressure ([importunity](importunity.md)).
