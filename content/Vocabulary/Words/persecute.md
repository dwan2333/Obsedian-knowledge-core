# persecute

> [!definition] /ˈpɜːsɪkjuːt/ · *verb*
> To subject someone to hostility and ill-treatment, especially over a long period and because of their identity or beliefs. (You met it as **persecuted**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The sect was **persecuted** for centuries, driven from one country to the next.
> - She felt **persecuted** by the constant, petty fault-finding.
> - Regimes that **persecute** their critics rarely survive the telling of it.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and strongly negative — it names sustained, unjust cruelty, usually aimed at a group for who they are or what they believe. Heavier than "harass"; the sufferer who dies for the cause becomes a [martyr](martyr.md). (Don't confuse with *prosecute*, to bring to trial.)
