# piquant

> [!definition] /ˈpiːkənt/ · *adjective*
> Pleasantly sharp or spicy to the taste; pleasantly stimulating or intriguing to the mind.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **piquant** sauce of capers and lemon lifted the plain fish.
> - There was a **piquant** detail in the story that made everyone lean in.
> - She had a **piquant** wit — just enough sting to keep you on your toes.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and appreciative, always positive. The adjective to [piquancy](piquancy.md)'s noun: a welcome sharpness that *adds interest*, never an unpleasant one. Pairs with food, flavour, wit, and detail.
