# quarrelsome

> [!definition] /ˈkwɒrəlsəm/ · *adjective*
> Given to arguing and picking fights; quick to quarrel.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Drink made him **quarrelsome**, and the evening usually ended in shouting.
> - The two **quarrelsome** neighbours took each other to court over a hedge.
> - She was tired and **quarrelsome**, finding fault with everything.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and negative — it describes a settled disposition toward conflict, not a single argument. Reach for it to characterise someone whose first instinct is to dispute.
