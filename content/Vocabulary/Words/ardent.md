# ardent

> [!definition] /ˈɑːdənt/ · *adjective*
> Burning with passion, enthusiasm, or conviction; intensely devoted. (Literally, *burning* — from Latin *ardere*, to burn.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - An **ardent** admirer of her work, he had read every line she ever published.
> - They were **ardent** in their faith, rising before dawn to pray.
> - His **ardent** speeches could move a hostile crowd to cheers within minutes.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and warm — almost always positive or at least sympathetic. It dignifies strong feeling as noble *fire*, where [fanatic](fanatic.md) condemns it as excess. Pairs with love, faith, devotion, and belief.
