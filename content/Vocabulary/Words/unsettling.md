# unsettling

> [!definition] /ʌnˈsɛtlɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Causing unease or anxiety; quietly disturbing one's sense of calm or security.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - There was something **unsettling** about how quiet the house had become.
> - The report made for **unsettling** reading over breakfast.
> - She gave an **unsettling** smile that didn't reach her eyes.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and negative, but mild and creeping rather than alarming — it names the faint wrongness that disturbs without quite frightening. Where [ominous](ominous.md) threatens, *unsettling* merely unnerves.
