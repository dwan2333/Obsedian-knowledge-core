# rapidity

> [!definition] /rəˈpɪdɪti/ · *noun*
> Great speed; swiftness — the quality or rate of being rapid.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **rapidity** of the change caught everyone off guard; the town was unrecognisable in a decade.
> - She solved the puzzles with a **rapidity** that left the examiners blinking.
> - The fire spread with frightening **rapidity** through the dry brush.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral; the abstract-noun counterpart to *fast*. In everyday speech people say "how quickly," but in writing *rapidity* names the speed itself as a quality. *Celerity* is its more literary cousin.
