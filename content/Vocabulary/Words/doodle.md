# doodle

> [!definition] /ˈduːdəl/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To scribble or draw absent-mindedly, especially while thinking about something else. As a noun, such a drawing. (You met it as **doodled**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She **doodled** little spirals in the margin all through the meeting.
> - His notebook was covered in **doodles** of rockets and robots.
> - I **doodle** when I'm on the phone, without noticing I'm doing it.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday, informal, and neutral-to-affectionate — it names idle, unselfconscious drawing, the by-product of a wandering mind. Light and a little endearing; carries no sense of skill or seriousness.
