# inmate

> [!definition] /ˈɪnmeɪt/ · *noun*
> A person confined to an institution — a prison, asylum, or hospital. (Archaically, simply one of the occupants or dwellers of a house.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The prison's **inmates** were let into the yard for one hour each day.
> - The asylum's **inmates** shuffled along the corridor in their grey gowns.
> - In the older sense, every **inmate** of the great house, from master to scullion, gathered for prayers.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and institutional in the modern sense (prison, asylum). The older, broader sense of "household occupant" is now archaic and survives mainly in classic fiction — worth knowing so the period usage doesn't mislead.
