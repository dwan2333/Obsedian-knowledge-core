# wearying

> [!definition] /ˈwɪəriɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Causing tiredness, especially the slow, dull fatigue that comes from something tedious, drawn-out, or relentless. (Also the present participle of *to weary* — to grow or make tired.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The meeting dragged through a **wearying** list of small grievances no one cared about.
> - After a **wearying** day of delayed trains, she wanted nothing but silence.
> - There is something **wearying** about constant cheerfulness when you do not share it.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and mildly literary, negative — it stresses the draining effect of dullness or repetition rather than sheer hard work. Choose it over *tiring* when the fatigue is as much of the spirit as the body.
