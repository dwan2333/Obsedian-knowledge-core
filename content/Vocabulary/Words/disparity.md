# disparity

> [!definition] /dɪˈspærɪti/ · *noun*
> A great difference or inequality between things, especially an unfair one.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **disparity** between the two salaries was impossible to justify.
> - There is a glaring **disparity** in how the rule is applied to rich and poor.
> - Such a **disparity** in size made the contest a foregone conclusion.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral-to-critical, common in writing about economics, fairness, and statistics. It names not just a difference but a *consequential, often unjust* one. The opposite is *parity*.
