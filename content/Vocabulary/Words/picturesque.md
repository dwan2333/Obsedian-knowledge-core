# picturesque

> [!definition] /ˌpɪktʃəˈrɛsk/ · *adjective*
> Visually charming or striking, as if it belonged in a picture — especially of scenery, towns, and buildings. Of language, vivid and colourful — *a picturesque turn of phrase*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **picturesque** fishing village, all crooked lanes and bright boats, is overrun with painters each summer.
> - He told the story in **picturesque** detail, sparing us nothing of the storm.
> - It looks **picturesque** in photographs, but living there through the winter is another matter.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Positive and slightly aesthetic in tone, common in travel writing and description. A faint caution: it can imply *charming to look at but not practical*. The opposite of [prosaic](prosaic.md) — vivid and pictorial where prosaic is flat and plain.
