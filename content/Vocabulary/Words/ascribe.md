# ascribe

> [!definition] /əˈskraɪb/ · *verb*
> To attribute something to a cause, source, or author; to credit a quality or statement to someone. (You met it as **ascribed**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Historians **ascribe** the collapse to a mix of drought and debt.
> - The quotation is usually **ascribed** to Twain, though he never said it.
> - She **ascribed** his rudeness to nerves rather than malice.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, common in scholarly and analytical writing. To *ascribe* is to assign a cause or origin, often tentatively or by judgement. Distinguish from *subscribe* and *describe*; the related noun is *ascription*.
