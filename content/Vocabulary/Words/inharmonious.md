# inharmonious

> [!definition] /ˌɪnhɑːˈməʊniəs/ · *adjective*
> Lacking harmony; discordant, clashing, or not in agreement — of sounds, colours, or relationships.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The choir's **inharmonious** opening notes made the conductor wince.
> - It was an **inharmonious** household, full of slammed doors and long silences.
> - The new wing's glass façade looked **inharmonious** beside the old stone hall.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, neutral-to-negative. The plain negation of *harmonious*, useful for clashing sounds, mismatched designs, or strained relations alike. A touch elevated; "clashing" or "discordant" is the everyday choice.
