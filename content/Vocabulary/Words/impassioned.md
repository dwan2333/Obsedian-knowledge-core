# impassioned

> [!definition] /ɪmˈpæʃənd/ · *adjective*
> Filled with and expressing intense feeling; passionate and fervent, especially in speech.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She made an **impassioned** plea for the refugees before the assembly.
> - His **impassioned** letter ran to twelve pages.
> - The debate grew **impassioned**, voices rising on both sides.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and literary, generally positive or at least sympathetic — it dignifies strong feeling as eloquent and heartfelt, most often of speeches, appeals, and arguments. Warmer and more articulate than merely "emotional."
