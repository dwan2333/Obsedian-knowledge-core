# revolting

> [!definition] /rɪˈvəʊltɪŋ/ · *adjective*
> Causing disgust; deeply offensive or repulsive. (Distinct from the unrelated sense of *revolting* = rebelling.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **revolting** smell of rot met them at the cellar door.
> - The conditions in the workhouse were frankly **revolting**.
> - He found the whole spectacle **revolting** and left before the end.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and emphatically negative — a strong word for physical or moral disgust, stronger than "unpleasant" or "nasty." Reach for it when something turns the stomach, literally or figuratively. (Context separates it cleanly from "revolting" in the sense of rising in revolt.)
