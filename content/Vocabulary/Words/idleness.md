# idleness

> [!definition] /ˈaɪdəlnəs/ · *noun*
> The state of being idle — not working, busy, or in use; habitual avoidance of activity or employment. It can mean simple inactivity or, more pointedly, laziness as a failing.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A long summer of enforced **idleness** left him restless and short of money.
> - The proverb warns that **idleness** is the root of much mischief.
> - She could not bear **idleness**, and filled every spare hour with some small project.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday but slightly elevated, mildly negative — it leans toward reproach more than the neutral *inactivity*. Use it when you want a faint moral colouring; choose *leisure* or *rest* when the inactivity is welcome and unblamed.
