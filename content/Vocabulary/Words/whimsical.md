# whimsical

> [!definition] /ˈwɪmzɪkəl/ · *adjective*
> Playfully fanciful or quaint; acting on sudden whims rather than reason. (You met it as **whimsically**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The garden was full of **whimsical** touches — a sundial that told the wrong time, a door to nowhere.
> - He made his decisions **whimsically**, by coin-toss as often as by thought.
> - Her **whimsical** humour delighted the children and unsettled the examiners.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-positive and light. *Whimsical* is the charming, playful face of changeability — fanciful and amusing, where [caprice](caprice.md) leans toward the unreliable. Pairs with humour, fancy, and gentle eccentricity.
