# frantic

> [!definition] /ˈfræntɪk/ · *adjective*
> Wild with fear, anxiety, or hurry; done in a desperate, disordered rush.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - There was a **frantic** scramble for the exits when the alarm sounded.
> - She made **frantic** phone calls all night trying to find him.
> - The last hour before the deadline was **frantic**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and vivid, negative-leaning — it conveys panic-driven haste, action robbed of calm by fear or urgency. Close to [frenzied](frenzied.md), but more often about anxious desperation than wild excitement.
