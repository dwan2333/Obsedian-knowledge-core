# endow

> [!definition] /ɪnˈdaʊ/ · *verb*
> To provide someone or something with a quality, ability, or asset; to give a permanent fund of income to an institution. (You met it as **endowed** — *endowed with talent*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Nature had **endowed** her with both beauty and a sharp tongue.
> - A wealthy alumnus **endowed** the new library.
> - The constitution **endows** every citizen with these rights.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral-to-positive. In the "provide with a quality" sense it is literary (*endowed with*), often of gifts seen as innate or bestowed; in the financial sense it is the precise term for funding a chair, a college, or a charity in perpetuity.
