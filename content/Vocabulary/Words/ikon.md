# ikon

> [!definition] /ˈaɪkɒn/ · *noun*
> A painted image of Christ, the Virgin, or a saint, venerated in the Eastern Orthodox Church. (A variant spelling of *icon*, used especially in this religious sense and in older translations of Russian literature.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A small **ikon** of the Virgin hung in the corner, a lamp burning beneath it.
> - The old woman crossed herself before the **ikon** and went out.
> - The monastery's most ancient **ikon** was said to work miracles.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and specific, with a devotional, Orthodox flavour — and a period feel, common in nineteenth-century Russian novels. The spelling *icon* now dominates (and has taken on the broader "symbol/celebrity" sense); *ikon* keeps the narrower religious meaning.
