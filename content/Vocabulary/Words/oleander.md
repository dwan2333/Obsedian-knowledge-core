# oleander

> [!definition] /ˌəʊliˈændə/ · *noun*
> An evergreen Mediterranean shrub with clusters of fragrant white, pink, or red flowers — and highly poisonous in every part.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Pink **oleander** bloomed all along the dusty roadside.
> - She warned the children never to chew the leaves of the **oleander**.
> - The villa's terrace was screened by a hedge of white **oleander**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete — the precise name of the plant. It carries a quiet double note in literature: lush, fragrant beauty that is also deadly, which writers often exploit. No plainer synonym; it is simply the flower's name.
