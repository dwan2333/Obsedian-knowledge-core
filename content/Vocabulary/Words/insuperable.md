# insuperable

> [!definition] /ɪnˈsuːpərəbəl/ · *adjective*
> (Of a difficulty, barrier, or obstacle) impossible to overcome. Used almost always of problems, not of people or armies.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The language barrier proved **insuperable**, and the negotiations collapsed within a week.
> - What looked like an **insuperable** lead in the polls evaporated in the final month.
> - For a small studio, the cost of the licence was simply **insuperable**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and somewhat literary, with a note of finality. Stronger and more elegant than "impossible to get past"; reserve it for genuinely decisive obstacles. Its near-twin *insurmountable* is interchangeable and a touch more common.
