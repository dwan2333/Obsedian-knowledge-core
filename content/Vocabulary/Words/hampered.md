# hampered

> [!definition] /ˈhæmpəd/ (HAM-perd) · *verb (past tense and past participle of* hamper*), often used as an adjective*
> Held back, slowed, or made more difficult by an obstacle or restriction. To *hamper* is to hinder movement or progress without stopping it entirely. (The noun *hamper*, a large basket, is an unrelated word.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Rescue efforts were **hampered** by heavy snow and blocked roads.
> - Without a laptop of his own, his coding practice was badly **hampered**.
> - The team was **hampered** by injuries for most of the season.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and common, especially in news and reports, usually in the pattern *hampered by …*. It means slowed or made harder, not stopped: weaker than *blocked* or *thwarted*. Plainer synonyms are *held back* and *slowed down*; *impeded* is the more formal twin.
