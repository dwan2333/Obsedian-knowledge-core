# installment

> [!definition] /ɪnˈstɔːlmənt/ · *noun*
> A part-payment in a series by which a debt is gradually settled; also, one part of a story or programme published or broadcast in sequence.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She paid for the sofa in six monthly **installments**.
> - Readers waited a year for the next **installment** of the serialized novel.
> - The final **installment** of the loan cleared the balance at last.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and everyday, with a foot in finance and publishing. Precise where *payment* or *part* would be vague. (British spelling: *instalment*.)
