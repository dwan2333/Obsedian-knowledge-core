# elopement

> [!definition] /ɪˈloʊpmənt/ · *noun*
> The act of running away secretly with a lover, especially in order to marry without parents' consent or a public ceremony.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Their **elopement** scandalised both families, who learned of the marriage only by letter.
> - She had dreamed of a grand wedding, but a quiet **elopement** was all they could afford.
> - The novel turns on a midnight **elopement** that goes disastrously wrong.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-romantic and slightly old-fashioned, with a whiff of drama and defiance. It implies secrecy and a breach of expectation — the couple is escaping something (a family, a custom, a delay).
