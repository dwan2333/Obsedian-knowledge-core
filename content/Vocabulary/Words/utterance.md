# utterance

> [!definition] /ˈʌtərəns/ · *noun*
> Something spoken; a word, remark, or vocal expression. Also the act of speaking (*to give utterance to a feeling*). (You met it as **utterances**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Her every public **utterance** was scrutinised by the press.
> - He gave **utterance** to a groan of pure despair.
> - The prophet's cryptic **utterances** were debated for centuries.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary — a weightier, more deliberate word than "remark" or "statement," often used of pronouncements treated as significant. Common in linguistics (a unit of speech) and in elevated description of what someone says.
