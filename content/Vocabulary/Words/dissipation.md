# dissipation

> [!definition] /ˌdɪsɪˈpeɪʃən/ · *noun*
> Dissolute, self-indulgent living — drinking, gambling, and pleasure pursued to the point of waste; the squandering of money, health, or time. (Also, more neutrally, the scattering or dispersal of something.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Years of **dissipation** had ruined both his fortune and his face.
> - The estate slid from his father's care into the son's careless **dissipation**.
> - After a winter of **dissipation** in the capital, he returned home pale and penniless.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, strongly disapproving. The abstract noun for a life of [debauchery](debauchery.md); the adjective for the person is [dissipated](dissipated.md). Reach for it to name not a single binge but a whole pattern of self-ruin.
