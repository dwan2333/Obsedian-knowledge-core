# entreat

> [!definition] /ɪnˈtriːt/ · *verb*
> To ask someone earnestly or anxiously to do something; to beg.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She **entreated** them to reconsider before it was too late.
> - He wrote to **entreat** his father's forgiveness.
> - "Stay a little longer," she **entreated**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, earnest but a shade gentler than [beseech](beseech.md) — pressing without the note of desperation. Suited to courteous, heartfelt appeals in elevated prose.
