# inflict

> [!definition] /ɪnˈflɪkt/ · *verb*
> To cause something unpleasant — pain, suffering, damage, or punishment — to be endured by someone. (You met it as **inflicting**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The storm **inflicted** terrible damage on the coastal towns.
> - He seemed to enjoy **inflicting** small humiliations on his staff.
> - I won't **inflict** my holiday photos on you, I promise.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative — to *inflict* is to impose something harmful or unwelcome on a victim, from wounds to boredom. Pairs with pain, damage, defeat, and (lightly, self-deprecatingly) one's own tedious company.
