# flock

> [!definition] /flɒk/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To gather or move somewhere in large numbers. As a noun, a group of birds or sheep — or, figuratively, a congregation under a pastor. (You met it as **flocked**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Tourists **flock** to the village every summer for the festival.
> - Crows **flocked** to the freshly ploughed field.
> - The priest worried about his dwindling **flock**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral — vivid for crowds drawn to something in numbers (*flock to*). The noun carries a gentle pastoral or religious overtone (a shepherd's flock, a minister's congregation). Mild, common, and warm rather than critical.
