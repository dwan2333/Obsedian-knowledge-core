# fanatic

> [!definition] /fəˈnætɪk/ · *noun (also adjective)*
> A person filled with excessive, uncritical zeal, especially for a religious or political cause.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He was a **fanatic** for the cause, and would hear no argument that questioned it.
> - The sect's **fanatics** smashed the icons they had once knelt before.
> - A health **fanatic**, she weighed every mouthful and ran in any weather.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and almost always negative — it condemns devotion that has lost its judgment. The difference from [ardent](ardent.md) is the loss of balance: a fanatic's zeal overrides reason, proportion, and often kindness. (Casually, "a fanatic for X" can simply mean a keen enthusiast.)
