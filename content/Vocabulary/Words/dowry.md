# dowry

> [!definition] /ˈdaʊri/ · *noun*
> The money, goods, or property a bride brings to her husband or his family at marriage.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Without a **dowry**, her chances of a good match were slim, whatever her other virtues.
> - The negotiations over the **dowry** lasted longer than the courtship.
> - He married her for the land that came as her **dowry**, not for love.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and largely historical or anthropological in modern use, though the custom persists in parts of the world. A precise term for writing about marriage as a social and economic arrangement.
