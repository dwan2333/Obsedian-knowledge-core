# yearn

> [!definition] /jɜːn/ · *verb*
> To feel a deep, lasting longing for something, especially something absent, lost, or out of reach. Used with *for* or *to* — *yearn for home*, *yearn to be free*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - After three years abroad she **yearned** for the smell of her mother's kitchen.
> - He **yearned** to be taken seriously, and worked twice as hard as anyone to earn it.
> - The song is full of **yearning** for a youth that is not coming back.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Literary-leaning and tender in tone. Stronger than *want* and sadder than *desire*, because it implies the thing is far away or may never come. In everyday speech *long for* or *miss* usually does the job; reach for *yearn* when the wanting is deep and aching. The noun is *yearning*.
