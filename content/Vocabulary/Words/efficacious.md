# efficacious

> [!definition] /ˌɛfɪˈkeɪʃəs/ · *adjective*
> Producing the intended result; effective. Said of remedies, treatments, methods, and measures.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Aspirin proved **efficacious** in preventing a second heart attack.
> - Economists doubted that a tax cut would be **efficacious** against inflation.
> - Of all the cures her grandmother swore by, only the ginger tea seemed even mildly **efficacious**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and a little stiff; in most writing *effective* is the plain choice. Like its noun, *efficacy*, it describes things, not people: a person is *effective* or *competent*, never efficacious. Keep it apart from *efficient*, which means doing something with little waste. The opposites are *ineffective*, *ineffectual*, and *futile*.
