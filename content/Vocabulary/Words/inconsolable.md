# inconsolable

> [!definition] /ˌɪnkənˈsəʊləbəl/ · *adjective*
> So grief-stricken that no comfort can ease the pain; impossible to console.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - When the dog died, the children were **inconsolable** for days.
> - She stood **inconsolable** at the graveside, deaf to every gentle word.
> - He was **inconsolable** at the loss, not of the money, but of his good name.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-literary, conveying the deepest grade of grief. Stronger than "upset" or "heartbroken" — it specifies that *consolation has failed*. Reach for it at the extremity of sorrow, where comfort is useless.
