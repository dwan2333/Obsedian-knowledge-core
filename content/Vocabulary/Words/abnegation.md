# abnegation

> [!definition] /ˌæbnɪˈɡeɪʃən/ · *noun*
> The renouncing or denial of something, especially the giving up of one's own interests, comforts, or desires; self-denial.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The monastic life demanded a complete **abnegation** of personal wealth.
> - Her years of quiet self-**abnegation** had left nothing in the household for herself.
> - There is a fine line between humility and the **abnegation** that erases a person entirely.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and weighty — usually admiring of a noble self-denial, but capable of a darker note when self-erasure goes too far. At home in religious, moral, and psychological writing; "self-denial" or "self-sacrifice" is the plainer cousin.
