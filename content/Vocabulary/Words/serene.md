# serene

> [!definition] /sɪˈriːn/ · *adjective*
> Calm, peaceful, and untroubled; undisturbed by agitation or distress.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She remained **serene** through the chaos, as though the shouting reached her from far away.
> - The lake lay **serene** and mirror-still in the early light.
> - His **serene** confidence never wavered, even as the deadline collapsed around him.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish, literary, and positive — a deep, settled calm, grander and more composed than "relaxed." It suits faces, landscapes, and temperaments alike. The far pole from [ecstatic](ecstatic.md) rapture and from all agitation.
