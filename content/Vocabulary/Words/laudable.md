# laudable

> [!definition] /ˈlɔːdəbəl/ · *adjective*
> Deserving praise; commendable — usually of aims, efforts, or intentions.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Her motives were **laudable**, even if the plan itself was hopeless.
> - The scheme had the **laudable** goal of feeding every child a hot lunch.
> - It is **laudable** to try, but trying is not the same as succeeding.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, positive, but often faintly qualified — *laudable* frequently praises the intention while hinting that the result fell short ("laudable but unworkable"). Reach for it to commend an aim more than an achievement.
