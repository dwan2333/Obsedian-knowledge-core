# dread

> [!definition] /drɛd/ · *noun (also verb)*
> Great fear or apprehension about something about to happen. As a verb, to anticipate with deep unease. (You met it as **dreaded**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A cold **dread** settled over her as the footsteps came nearer.
> - He **dreaded** the conversation he knew he could no longer put off.
> - The **dreaded** day of the examination arrived at last.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and strongly negative — fear deepened by anticipation, the sinking certainty that something bad is coming. As an adjective (*dreaded*), it marks a thing as fearfully unwelcome.
