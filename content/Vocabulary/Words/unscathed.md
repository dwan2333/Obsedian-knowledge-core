# unscathed

> [!definition] /ʌnˈskeɪðd/ · *adjective*
> Without suffering any injury or harm; completely unhurt.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The car was wrecked, but she walked away **unscathed**.
> - His reputation came through the scandal surprisingly **unscathed**.
> - Few of the old buildings survived the fire **unscathed**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral-to-positive — it marks a welcome escape from expected harm, literal or figurative (a body, a reputation, a career). Almost always used of coming *through* a danger; pairs with accidents, attacks, and crises.
