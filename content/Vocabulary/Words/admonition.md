# admonition

> [!definition] /ˌædməˈnɪʃən/ · *noun*
> A firm but usually gentle warning or reproof; an earnest caution against a fault.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Despite his mother's **admonition**, he climbed the wall anyway.
> - The judge let her off with a stern **admonition**.
> - The essay closes on an **admonition** against complacency.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, mildly negative — it reproves or cautions, but with a counselling, corrective tone rather than outright anger. Softer than a rebuke, weightier than a hint. The reproving twin of the rousing [exhortation](exhortation.md).
