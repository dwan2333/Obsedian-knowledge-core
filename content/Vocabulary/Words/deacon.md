# deacon

> [!definition] /ˈdiːkən/ · *noun*
> An ordained minister or lay officer of the Christian church ranking just below a priest, charged with assisting in worship and with practical and charitable duties.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **deacon** carried the collection plate down the aisle and laid it at the altar.
> - Before his ordination as a priest, he served two years as a **deacon**.
> - As **deacon**, she ran the parish food bank and visited the sick each week.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral; a precise ecclesiastical title rather than a literary flourish. The exact role varies by denomination — clergy in some churches, an elected lay leader in others — so the surrounding context usually does the defining.
