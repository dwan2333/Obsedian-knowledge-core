# custodian

> [!definition] /kʌˈstəʊdiən/ · *noun*
> A person who has responsibility for looking after or protecting something; a keeper, guardian, or caretaker. (In US usage, also a building janitor.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The museum sees itself as the **custodian** of the nation's memory.
> - As **custodian** of the estate, she kept every account to the penny.
> - The night **custodian** locked up and switched off the lights.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-respectful — it dignifies the role of guardianship, whether of treasures, traditions, or a building. Weightier than "caretaker"; often used figuratively (custodian of values, of the law, of a legacy).
