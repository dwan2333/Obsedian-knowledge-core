# ecstasy

> [!definition] /ˈɛkstəsi/ · *noun*
> An overwhelming feeling of great happiness or joyful excitement; a trance or rapture, especially of a mystical or religious kind.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She closed her eyes in **ecstasy** at the first note of the aria.
> - The saint's writings describe an **ecstasy** in which the self seemed to dissolve.
> - The crowd was in a state of near-**ecstasy** as the band took the stage.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-to-literary and intensely positive — joy or rapture at its uttermost, often with a transcendent, out-of-body flavour. The noun behind [ecstatic](ecstatic.md). (Capitalised, *Ecstasy* is also the drug MDMA — context keeps them apart.)
