# absolution

> [!definition] /ˌæbsəˈluːʃən/ · *noun*
> Formal release from guilt or sin, especially the forgiveness pronounced by a priest; more broadly, any clearing of blame.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The dying man begged the priest for **absolution**.
> - She wanted not just understanding but **absolution**, to be told she had done no wrong.
> - The report stopped short of granting the company full **absolution**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, with a strong religious resonance; neutral-to-solemn. In its sacramental sense it is precise (the priest's forgiveness after confession); figuratively, it lends weight to any release from guilt or blame.
