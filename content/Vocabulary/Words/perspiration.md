# perspiration

> [!definition] /ˌpɜːspəˈreɪʃən/ · *noun*
> Sweat; the moisture given off through the skin. Figuratively, hard effort (*genius is one per cent inspiration, ninety-nine per cent perspiration*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - **Perspiration** ran down his face as he heaved the last crate up the stairs.
> - The room was close and airless, and she felt the first beads of **perspiration**.
> - The result owed less to talent than to sheer **perspiration**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and polite — the genteel word for sweat, preferred in careful or delicate writing ("sweat" being blunter and more physical). The figurative "hard work" sense is mildly approving of plain effort.
