# indissoluble

> [!definition] /ˌɪndɪˈsɒljʊbəl/ · *adjective*
> Unable to be broken, undone, or destroyed; permanent — especially of a bond, union, or tie.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - They believed marriage to be an **indissoluble** union, for life and beyond.
> - The two ideas are bound together in an **indissoluble** knot.
> - War forged an **indissoluble** friendship between the survivors.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and emphatic — it declares a bond unbreakable, often with solemn or legal weight (marriages, alliances, principles). Stronger than "lasting"; it denies that the tie *can* be broken at all.
