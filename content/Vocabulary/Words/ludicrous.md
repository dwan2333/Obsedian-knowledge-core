# ludicrous

> [!definition] /ˈluːdɪkrəs/ · *adjective*
> So absurd or unreasonable as to be laughable; ridiculous.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The idea that one part-timer could do the work of five is frankly **ludicrous**.
> - He turned up to the funeral in a **ludicrous** purple suit and seemed baffled by the stares.
> - Prices have reached **ludicrous** heights — a coffee now costs more than lunch used to.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> General-register and emphatically negative, with an edge of scorn or disbelief. Stronger than *silly*: it says the thing is so far beyond reason that it invites laughter. Pairs naturally with *absurd* and *preposterous*.
