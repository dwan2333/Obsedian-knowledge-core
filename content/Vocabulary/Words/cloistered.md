# cloistered

> [!definition] /ˈklɔɪstəd/ · *adjective*
> Secluded from the outside world, as in a monastery; sheltered and shut away from ordinary life. (You met it as **cloistered**; the root is *cloister*, a covered monastic walkway.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He had led a **cloistered** life, and the noise and grasping of the city bewildered him.
> - The scholars worked in **cloistered** calm, untouched by the politics outside the walls.
> - She resented her **cloistered** upbringing, which had left her unprepared for anything.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, often faintly critical — a *cloistered* life is protected but also naive, cut off from real experience. Reach for it when "sheltered" needs the added flavour of monastic withdrawal.
