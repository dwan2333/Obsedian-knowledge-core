# decadent

> [!definition] /ˈdɛkədənt/ · *adjective*
> Marked by moral or cultural decline, especially through excessive self-indulgence and luxury. Loosely and playfully, indulgently rich (*a decadent dessert*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The court had grown **decadent**, more interested in pleasures than in governing.
> - Critics called the novel **decadent** — beautiful, perfumed, and rotten at the core.
> - They ended the meal with a **decadent** chocolate cake nobody had room for.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and disapproving in its serious sense (decline through indulgence); light and approving in its playful modern sense (luxuriously rich). Context decides which — the dessert is a treat, the empire is doomed.
