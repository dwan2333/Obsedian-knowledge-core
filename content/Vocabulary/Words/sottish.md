# sottish

> [!definition] /ˈsɒtɪʃ/ · *adjective*
> Stupefied or foolish from habitual drunkenness; befitting a drunkard. (From *sot*, an archaic word for a habitual drinker.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He sank into a **sottish** stupor by every evening, and his work fell to ruin.
> - There was something **sottish** in the slack grin and the unfocused eyes.
> - The tavern was full of **sottish** old men reliving battles they had never fought.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and contemptuous — now somewhat archaic. It paints not just drunkenness but the dull, degraded foolishness that habitual drinking breeds. A vivid period word; in plain speech "drunken" or "boozy" carries the bare sense.
