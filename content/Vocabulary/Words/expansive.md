# expansive

> [!definition] /ɪkˈspænsɪv/ · *adjective*
> (Of a person or mood) genial, open, and communicative — warmed into free, generous talk. (Also, more literally, wide-ranging or covering a large area.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - After dinner and a glass of port he grew **expansive**, and the stories flowed.
> - She was in an **expansive** mood and waved away every objection with a laugh.
> - The book takes an **expansive** view, sweeping across three centuries.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and positive in the "genial" sense — it pictures a person opening up, becoming warm and free. (The "wide-ranging" sense is neutral and descriptive.) Reach for the social sense when someone relaxes into generous, talkative warmth.
