# incarnation

> [!definition] /ˌɪnkɑːˈneɪʃən/ · *noun*
> A living embodiment of a quality or idea (*the incarnation of evil*); a particular version or form in which something or someone appears (*the company in its latest incarnation*); in religion, a god taking bodily form.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - To his enemies he was the very **incarnation** of greed.
> - In an earlier **incarnation**, the building had been a textile mill.
> - The doctrine of the **Incarnation** holds that God became man.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-literary. In the "embodiment" sense it is emphatic (someone *is* a quality made flesh); in the "version" sense it is common in writing about brands, careers, and reinventions; capitalised, it names the Christian doctrine. Versatile across all three.
