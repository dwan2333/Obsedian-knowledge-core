# vigorous

> [!definition] /ˈvɪɡərəs/ · *adjective*
> Full of strength and energy; done with force, effort, and determination. Applies to bodies (*a vigorous stride*), efforts (*a vigorous defence*), and growth (*a vigorous plant*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Despite his age, he took a **vigorous** walk through the hills every morning before breakfast.
> - The proposal met **vigorous** opposition, with three committee members speaking against it at once.
> - Pruning in spring encourages more **vigorous** growth later in the year.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-positive, at home in everyday and formal writing alike. It conveys not just energy but *forceful, effective* energy — a vigorous effort is one being pressed hard. The direct opposite of [puny](puny.md).
