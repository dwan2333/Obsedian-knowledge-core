# mopey

> [!definition] /ˈməʊpi/ · *adjective*
> Gloomy and listless; sulkily low in spirits. (Informal; also spelled *mopy*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He's been **mopey** all week since the team lost.
> - Don't sit there looking **mopey** — come and join in.
> - The rainy holiday left the children **mopey** and bored.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and mildly dismissive — a light, everyday word for low, sulky spirits, gentler and more colloquial than [morose](morose.md). It suggests a passing gloom one could be coaxed out of, rather than a deep or dignified sorrow.
