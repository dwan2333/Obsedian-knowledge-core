# canary

> [!definition] /kəˈnɛəri/ · *noun*
> A small finch, usually bright yellow, kept as a cage bird for its song. (Slang: an informer who "sings" to the authorities.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **canary** sang in its cage by the window, indifferent to the gloom inside.
> - Miners once carried a **canary** underground as a living alarm for poison gas.
> - The gang suspected a **canary** in their midst long before the arrests began.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete for the bird. The "informer" sense is slang, chiefly in crime contexts (from the bird's singing). Note the proverbial *canary in the coal mine* — an early warning of danger.
