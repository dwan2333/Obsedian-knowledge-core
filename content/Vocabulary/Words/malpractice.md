# malpractice

> [!definition] /ˌmælˈpræktɪs/ · *noun*
> Improper, negligent, or unethical conduct by a professional — especially a doctor or lawyer — that falls short of the standards of their field.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The surgeon was sued for **malpractice** after the operation went wrong.
> - The report uncovered widespread financial **malpractice** at the bank.
> - Medical **malpractice** insurance costs some doctors a fortune.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and negative, with a legal edge — it names professional wrongdoing serious enough to breach duty, whether through negligence, incompetence, or misconduct. Most common of medicine, law, and finance.
