# proliferation

> [!definition] /prəˌlɪfəˈreɪʃən/ · *noun*
> A rapid increase in number or spread; the multiplication of something, often uncontrolled.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The treaty aimed to halt the **proliferation** of nuclear weapons.
> - The **proliferation** of delivery apps has changed how cities eat.
> - A **proliferation** of small committees slowed every decision to a crawl.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral-to-negative — it names rapid, spreading increase, frequently with a note of concern (weapons, red tape, cells, apps). Cooler and more technical than "explosion"; common in policy, science, and analysis. The verb is *proliferate*.
