a# venture

> [!definition] /ˈvɛntʃə/ · *noun & verb*
> (Noun) a risky or daring undertaking, especially a business one. (Verb) to dare to do or say something; to risk going somewhere uncertain.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The new **venture** could make their fortune or ruin them.
> - No one dared **venture** out into the storm.
> - "Might I **venture** an opinion?" she asked.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral, with an undertone of courage and risk — to *venture* is to step beyond safety, whether into a forest, a market, or an unwelcome remark. Note the proverb: *nothing ventured, nothing gained*.
