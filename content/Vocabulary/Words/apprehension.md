# apprehension

> [!definition] /ˌæprɪˈhɛnʃən/ · *noun*
> Anxiety or dread about something that may happen. (Other senses: understanding or grasp of an idea; the act of arresting someone.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She waited outside the office with a growing sense of **apprehension**.
> - He read the results with **apprehension**, fearing the worst.
> - The detective's chief concern was the **apprehension** of the suspect before nightfall.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-negative in its main sense — a quieter, more anxious cousin of [dread](dread.md), heavier than "nerves." Note the two other senses (understanding; arrest), which context keeps distinct.
