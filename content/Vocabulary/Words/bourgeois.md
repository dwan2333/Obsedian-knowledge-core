# bourgeois

> [!definition] /ˈbʊəʒwɑː/ · *adjective (also noun)*
> Of the middle class, especially in its supposed materialism, respectability, and conventional values. Often dismissive: narrowly concerned with property and propriety.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The play mocked the **bourgeois** household, with its lace doilies and its terror of scandal.
> - He despised what he called their **bourgeois** caution — save, marry well, offend no one.
> - For all her radical talk, her tastes were thoroughly **bourgeois**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and usually disparaging — a favourite term of artists and radicals for everything conventional, materialistic, and safe. Neutral only in technical sociology; elsewhere it nearly always carries a sneer.
