# hew

> [!definition] /hjuː/ · *verb*
> To cut or chop with heavy blows, as with an axe; to shape (wood or stone) by cutting. In the phrase *hew to*, to conform to or adhere closely to (a rule, line, or principle). (You met it as **hewing**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - They **hewed** a clearing out of the forest by hand.
> - The steps were **hewn** from the living rock.
> - The new manager **hews** closely to the company's old traditions.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Literary-to-everyday and vivid in the "chop/carve" sense, with a rugged, physical, often biblical flavour (*hewers of wood*). The figurative *hew to* (adhere to) is formal and chiefly American. Note the irregular past participle *hewn*.
