# envoy

> [!definition] /ˈɛnvɔɪ/ (EN-voy), also /ˈɒnvɔɪ/ (ON-voy) · *noun*
> A messenger or representative sent on a mission, especially one sent by a government to negotiate or deliver a message abroad. In formal diplomacy, a rank just below ambassador. (A separate literary sense, also spelled *envoi*, is the short closing stanza of a poem.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The president sent a special **envoy** to the region to try to broker a ceasefire.
> - The UN **envoy** met each side separately before the talks resumed.
> - She became the family's **envoy**, carrying the peace offer to her estranged uncle.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral; at home in news about diplomacy and international affairs (*special envoy*, *peace envoy*, *trade envoy*). Used of a person acting for someone else, it adds a touch of ceremony. Plainer words are *representative* and *messenger*; *emissary* is a close synonym, often for a more secret or unofficial errand. From French *envoyé*, "sent", from *envoyer*, "to send".
