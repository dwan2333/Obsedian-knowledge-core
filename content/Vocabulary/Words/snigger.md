# snigger

> [!definition] /ˈsnɪɡər/ · *verb, also noun*
> To laugh in a half-suppressed, sly, and disrespectful way, often at someone's expense; (noun) such a laugh.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The boys began to **snigger** at the teacher's slip behind their hands.
> - She caught a **snigger** rippling through the back row.
> - It is rude to **snigger** when a guest mispronounces a name.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and negative; the laughter is mean and cowardly rather than open. Choose it over plain *laugh* to convey mockery muffled into a snicker. (Variant spelling: *snicker*.)
