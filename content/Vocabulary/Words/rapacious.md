# rapacious

> [!definition] /rəˈpeɪʃəs/ · *adjective*
> Aggressively greedy or grasping; given to seizing more and more, like a bird of prey. (From Latin *rapere*, to seize.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **rapacious** landlord raised the rent the moment he sensed a tenant could pay.
> - A **rapacious** appetite for land drove the whole bloody campaign.
> - Their **rapacious** lending stripped the farmers of everything within a generation.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and strongly condemning — it casts greed as *predatory*, a seizing and devouring rather than mere wanting. Heavier than "greedy"; reach for it when avarice has teeth.
