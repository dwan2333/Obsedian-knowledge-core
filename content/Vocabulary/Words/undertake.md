# undertake

> [!definition] /ˌʌndəˈteɪk/ · *verb*
> To commit oneself to and begin a task or responsibility; to formally promise to do something. (You met it as **undertaken**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She **undertook** to finish the translation by the end of the month.
> - The firm has **undertaken** a complete overhaul of the bridge.
> - No one was willing to **undertake** so thankless a job.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, common in business, legal, and careful writing — weightier than "start" or "do," carrying a sense of commitment and responsibility. To undertake something is to take it on as a duty.
