# oblivion

> [!definition] /əˈblɪviən/ · *noun*
> The state of being utterly forgotten; also, a state of unconsciousness or obliteration into which things pass.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Once-famous, the poet has sunk into complete **oblivion**.
> - He drank himself into **oblivion** night after night.
> - The empire crumbled and was swallowed by **oblivion**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, with a sombre, final ring — it names the void of being forgotten, lost, or blotted out. Pairs with sinking, fading, and consigning. Grander and bleaker than "obscurity," which can still be recovered from.
