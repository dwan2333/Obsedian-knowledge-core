# wits

> [!definition] /wɪts/ · *plural noun*
> The mental faculties; the power of quick, practical thinking and good sense — *keep your wits, frightened out of his wits, live by one's wits*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Lost in the forest after dark, she kept her **wits** and followed the stream downhill.
> - The shock nearly drove him out of his **wits**.
> - With no money and no friends in the city, he lived by his **wits** alone.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and idiomatic, common in everyday speech through fixed phrases. Distinct from singular *wit* (humour, verbal cleverness): your **wits** are your faculties of mind — your alertness, judgment, and sanity.
