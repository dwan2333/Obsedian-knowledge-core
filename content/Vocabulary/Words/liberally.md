# liberally

> [!definition] /ˈlɪbərəli/ · *adverb*
> Freely and generously; in large or ample amounts (*tipped liberally, seasoned liberally*). Also: in a broad-minded or unrestricted way (*interpreted liberally*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He gave **liberally** to every beggar on the road, until his own purse was empty.
> - Season the stew **liberally** with pepper; it can take more than you'd think.
> - The rules were applied **liberally**, with exceptions made for almost anyone who asked.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and widely usable. Note that *liberally* here means *freely / generously / abundantly* — quite separate from the political sense of "liberal." Tone is positive when it's about generosity, neutral when it's about quantity.
