# blubber

> [!definition] /ˈblʌbə/ · *verb*
> To weep noisily and uncontrollably, with sobs and a wet, blubbering face. (You met it as **blubbering**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Stop **blubbering** and tell me what actually happened.
> - The big man sat down on the step and **blubbered** like a child.
> - She **blubbered** through the whole apology until none of it was intelligible.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and faintly mocking — *blubber* belittles the crying as messy and undignified, where "weep" or "sob" would respect it. Reach for it when you mean to make the tears a little ridiculous.
