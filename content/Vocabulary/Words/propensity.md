# propensity

> [!definition] /prəˈpɛnsɪti/ · *noun*
> A natural inclination or tendency to behave in a particular way, often a regrettable one — *a propensity for exaggeration*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He had a **propensity** for spending money he did not yet have.
> - Her **propensity** to assume the worst made every delay a small catastrophe.
> - The breed has a known **propensity** toward illness of the hip.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, common in careful or analytical writing. It names a *settled leaning* in character or behaviour — more inherent than a habit, more lasting than a mood. Often, though not always, the tendency named is an unwelcome one.
