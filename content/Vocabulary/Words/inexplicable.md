# inexplicable

> [!definition] /ˌɪnɪkˈsplɪkəbəl/ · *adjective*
> Unable to be explained or accounted for; baffling.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - For some **inexplicable** reason, the engine started on the third try and ran fine.
> - Her sudden disappearance remained **inexplicable** to everyone who knew her.
> - There was an **inexplicable** chill in the room, though the windows were shut.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral — the precise word for what defies explanation, stronger than "puzzling." Where [enigmatic](enigmatic.md) hints at a mystery worth dwelling on, *inexplicable* simply marks the limit of understanding.
