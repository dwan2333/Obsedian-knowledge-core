# affront

> [!definition] /əˈfrʌnt/ · *noun (also verb)*
> An open insult or deliberate act of disrespect. As a verb, to insult openly, or to confront defiantly.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Being seated below the salt was a deliberate **affront**, and everyone present knew it.
> - She took his correction as a personal **affront** and never forgave it.
> - To leave the gift unopened would **affront** the very people he needed.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and weighty — an *affront* is a public, pointed insult to one's dignity or honour, graver than a mere slight. The word belongs to a world that cares about face and standing; it pairs with pride, honour, and dignity.
