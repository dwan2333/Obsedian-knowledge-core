# scrape

> [!definition] /skreɪp/ · *noun*
> An awkward or difficult predicament, usually one brought on by one's own foolishness — *to get into a scrape*. (Distinct from the literal verb, to scrape a surface.) (You met it as **scrapes**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - As a boy he was always in some **scrape** or other, and always talked his way out.
> - This was a worse **scrape** than usual: there was money missing, and his name on the ledger.
> - She had a gift for getting friends out of the **scrapes** they got themselves into.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and faintly indulgent — a *scrape* is a mess, but a smallish, often forgivable one, the sort a likeable rascal lands in. Lighter than "crisis" or "trouble"; it pairs naturally with youth, mischief, and narrow escapes.
