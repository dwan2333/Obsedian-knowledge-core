# pence

> [!definition] /pɛns/ · *noun*
> British coins or sums of small value; the plural of *penny* used for amounts (*it costs fifty pence*), as opposed to *pennies* for the individual coins.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She counted out the last few **pence** and found she was still short of the fare.
> - For a few **pence** more, the larger loaf was clearly the better bargain.
> - He hadn't a **pence** to his name by the end of the month.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and chiefly British. In translated nineteenth-century novels it often stands in for small foreign coins (kopecks, sous), evoking poverty counted out coin by coin. Note the idiom: *pence* for the value, *pennies* for the coins themselves.
