# morose

> [!definition] /məˈrəʊs/ · *adjective*
> Sullen, gloomy, and ill-humoured; withdrawn into bad temper.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He sat **morose** in the corner all evening, answering questions in single words.
> - The long grey winter left the whole household **morose** and snappish.
> - Defeat made her **morose** for weeks; nothing anyone said could rouse her.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-literary, firmly negative. It blends gloom with a touch of sourness — a morose person is not merely sad but *unsociable* in their sadness. The opposite of [merry](merry.md).
