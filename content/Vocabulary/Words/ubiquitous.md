# ubiquitous

> [!definition] /juːˈbɪkwɪtəs/ (yoo-BIK-wih-tus) · *adjective*
> Present, found, or seen everywhere. Noun: **ubiquity**.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Smartphones have become so **ubiquitous** that it's hard to remember how people met up without them.
> - The coffee chain's green logo is **ubiquitous** in the city's business districts.
> - Python is **ubiquitous** in data science, from quick notebooks to production pipelines.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-sounding but very common in journalism, business, and tech writing. Usually neutral; it can carry a note of weariness (*the ubiquitous selfie stick*). Plainer options are *everywhere*, *widespread*, and *all over the place*. From Latin *ubique*, "everywhere" (*ubi*, "where").
