# importunity

> [!definition] /ˌɪmpɔːrˈtuːnɪti/ · *noun*
> Persistent, pressing, often annoying demands or requests; the quality of asking again and again until the other person is worn down.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He finally gave in, less out of generosity than to escape her endless **importunity**.
> - The charity's success owed much to the cheerful **importunity** of its volunteers.
> - Wearied by the **importunity** of creditors, he sold the last of the furniture.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, mildly negative — it frames the asking as excessive and wearing. The adjective *importunate* describes the person or the request. A precise word for pressure applied through sheer persistence.
