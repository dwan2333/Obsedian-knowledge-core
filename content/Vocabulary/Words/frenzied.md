# frenzied

> [!definition] /ˈfrɛnzid/ · *adjective*
> Wildly excited or agitated; frantic and out of control.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - There was **frenzied** activity on the trading floor as the news broke.
> - The crowd worked itself into a **frenzied** state, screaming the speaker's name.
> - She made one last **frenzied** search of the house before giving up the keys for lost.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-literary, vivid and negative-leaning — *frenzied* stresses loss of control, whether from fear, excitement, or rage. Stronger than "hectic"; it suggests action that has slipped past reason.
