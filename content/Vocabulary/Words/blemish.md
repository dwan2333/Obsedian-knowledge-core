# blemish

> [!definition] /ˈblɛmɪʃ/ · *noun* · *verb*
> *Noun:* a small mark or flaw that spoils how something looks, especially on the skin (a spot, a pimple, a small scar). Figuratively, a flaw on an otherwise good record, reputation, or performance. *Verb:* to spoil the look or perfection of something, often in the passive (*his record was blemished by one bad year*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She dabbed concealer on a small **blemish** on her chin.
> - The apples were cheap because their skins were covered in **blemishes**.
> - A single parking fine was the only **blemish** on his record.
> - One late penalty **blemished** an otherwise perfect season.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and everyday; in skincare adverts it is the polite word for spots and pimples. In the figurative sense a blemish is small, and it implies the thing is otherwise good. A *flaw* is a fault in how something is made or works, while a blemish is about the surface or appearance. *Blot* and *stain* are stronger and often moral (*a blot on the family name*). *Unblemished* means spotless or perfect (*an unblemished record*).
