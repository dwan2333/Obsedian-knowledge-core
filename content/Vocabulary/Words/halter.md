# halter

> [!definition] /ˈhɔːltər/ · *noun*
> A rope or strap fitted round an animal's head to lead or tie it. By grim extension, the rope of a *hangman's noose* — and so, figuratively, the gallows or execution itself.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He slipped a **halter** over the colt and led it gently from the stall.
> - The condemned man was marched out with the **halter** already about his neck.
> - To wear the **halter** was, in the old phrase, to be bound for the gallows.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday in the animal sense; archaic and ominous in the hanging sense. It sits among the vocabulary of restraint — a *tether* or *yoke* binds the body, a *noose* takes the life — and carries that whole arc from mere control to fatal constraint.
