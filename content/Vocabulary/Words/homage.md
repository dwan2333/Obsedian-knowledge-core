# homage

> [!definition] /ˈhɒmɪdʒ/ · *noun*
> Honour or deep respect shown publicly; a tribute, especially one that openly acknowledges another's greatness or influence.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Thousands filed past the coffin to pay **homage** to the old leader.
> - The young director's film is an open **homage** to the masters who taught him.
> - The knights knelt to do **homage** to their new lord.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and reverent, positive — it dignifies respect into something ceremonial and public. In its feudal sense it meant a vassal's formal allegiance; in modern use, an admiring tribute, often artistic.
