# sedentary

> [!definition] /ˈsɛdəntəri/ · *adjective*
> Characterized by much sitting and little physical activity; (of work or a lifestyle) done seated. (Also, of animals, staying in one place rather than migrating.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **sedentary** job had left him stiff and out of shape.
> - Doctors warn against an increasingly **sedentary** way of life.
> - The species is **sedentary**, rarely straying from the reef where it hatched.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-negative — increasingly used with a health warning attached (*a sedentary lifestyle*). It names inactivity through sitting, of a job, a habit, or a creature. Cooler and more clinical than "lazy," which judges the person rather than the pattern.
