# caprice

> [!definition] /kəˈpriːs/ · *noun*
> A sudden, unpredictable change of mind or behaviour, governed by impulse rather than reason; a whim. Also the *tendency* to act on such whims — fickleness.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - On a **caprice**, she sold the flat and moved to a fishing village she had visited once.
> - The fortunes of the courtiers rose and fell with the **caprice** of the queen.
> - Fashion is ruled by **caprice**; last year's must-have is this year's embarrassment.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, leaning mildly negative — it implies the change is arbitrary and a little unreliable. The adjective *capricious* is the everyday workhorse; the noun *caprice* keeps a more elegant air.
