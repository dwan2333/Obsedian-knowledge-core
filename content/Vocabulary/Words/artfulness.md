# artfulness

> [!definition] /ˈɑːtfʊlnəs/ · *noun*
> Crafty cleverness; skill in cunning, deceit, or getting one's way by indirect means.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - With practiced **artfulness**, he steered the conversation away from the missing money.
> - There was an **artfulness** to her innocence that made him trust her less, not more.
> - The con relied on sheer **artfulness** — no force, no threats, only a perfectly told lie.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and disapproving. Unlike the praise in "artful" craftsmanship, *artfulness* of character implies sly manipulation. The cunning cousin of plain cleverness — admire it at your peril.
