# perverse

> [!definition] /pəˈvɜːs/ · *adjective*
> Stubbornly contrary; choosing to do the opposite of what is reasonable or expected, often just to be difficult.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - With **perverse** satisfaction, he did exactly what he'd been warned not to.
> - It would be **perverse** to refuse help now, after asking for it all week.
> - She took a **perverse** pleasure in the bad reviews, as if proving a point.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative. *Perverse* is stubbornness with a wilful, almost spiteful twist — not merely refusing to yield (as [obstinate](obstinate.md) does) but actively preferring the wrong or contrary course. Pairs with pleasure, satisfaction, and refusal.
