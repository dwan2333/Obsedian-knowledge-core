# earnestness

> [!definition] /ˈɜːnɪstnəs/ · *noun*
> Sincere and serious conviction; the quality of being deeply in earnest, with no trace of joking or pretence.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He spoke with an **earnestness** that silenced the laughter in the room.
> - There was a touching **earnestness** in the child's promise to do better.
> - Her **earnestness** about the cause won over even the sceptics.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and positive-to-neutral — it praises wholehearted seriousness and sincerity, though too much of it can verge on the humourless. The grave, sincere cousin of mere enthusiasm.
