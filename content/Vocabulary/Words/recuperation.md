# recuperation

> [!definition] /rɪˌkuːpəˈreɪʃən/ · *noun*
> Recovery from illness, injury, or exhaustion; the time spent getting one's health and strength back. Less often, the recovery of money or losses. The verb is *recuperate*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - After the operation she needed six weeks of **recuperation** at home.
> - The team's schedule left no time for **recuperation** between matches.
> - A weekend by the sea was all the **recuperation** he could afford.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral to slightly formal, at home in medical, sporting, and everyday talk about rest. *Recovery* is the plain general word; *recuperation* stresses the gradual rebuilding of strength. *Convalescence* is more formal and old-fashioned, the whole period of rest after a serious illness. For getting money back, the usual verb is *recoup* (*recoup the cost*).
