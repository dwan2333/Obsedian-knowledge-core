# cynicism

> [!definition] /ˈsɪnɪsɪzəm/ · *noun*
> A distrustful, sneering belief that people are motivated chiefly by self-interest; a habitual expectation of the worst in others.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Years in politics had hardened his idealism into weary **cynicism**.
> - She met the charity appeal with open **cynicism**, certain someone was being fleeced.
> - There is a lazy **cynicism** that mistakes distrust for wisdom.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and negative, though some wear it as a badge of clear-sightedness. *Cynicism* is more than doubt — it is settled contempt for others' motives. Distinct from healthy scepticism, which questions claims rather than condemning people.
