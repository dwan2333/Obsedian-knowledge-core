# grit

> [!definition] /ɡrɪt/ · *noun*
> Courage, resolve, and strength of character — the toughness to keep going through hardship (*true grit*). (Literally, small loose particles of stone or sand.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - It took real **grit** to finish the race on a broken toe.
> - Talent gets you noticed, but **grit** is what gets the work done.
> - She gritted her teeth and held on, all **grit** and no complaint.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and strongly positive in the "resolve" sense — it admires unglamorous, dogged toughness over mere talent or flash. A figurative cousin of plain [vigorous](vigorous.md) strength, located in the will rather than the body. (Literally, the stuff in your shoe.)
