# harem

> [!definition] /ˈhɛrəm/ · /ˈhɑːriːm/ · *noun*
> In some traditional Muslim households, the separate living quarters reserved for women — wives, concubines, and female servants — and, by extension, the women who lived there.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The palace **harem** was a world apart, with its own courtyards, hierarchies, and intrigues.
> - European paintings of the **harem** revealed more about the painters' fantasies than about real life.
> - Within the **harem**, the sultan's mother often held the greatest power of all.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and historical or cultural in proper use, but the word carries a heavy load of Orientalist cliché, so handle it with care and context. *Seraglio* is a near-equivalent term.
