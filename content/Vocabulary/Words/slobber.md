# slobber

> [!definition] /ˈslɒbə/ · *verb*
> To let saliva run from the mouth; to drool. By extension, to be mawkishly, embarrassingly effusive. (You met it as **slobbered**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The great dog **slobbered** happily over every visitor's trousers.
> - He **slobbered** his soup, and she had to look away.
> - The critics **slobbered** over the new novel, praise dripping from every line.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and unflattering — *slobber* is physically distasteful in its literal sense and mockingly contemptuous in its figurative one (gushing, undignified praise). Reach for it when you want the reader faintly repelled.
