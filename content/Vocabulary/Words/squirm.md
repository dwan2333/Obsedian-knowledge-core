# squirm

> [!definition] /skwɜːm/ · *verb*
> To wriggle or twist the body, especially from discomfort, nervousness, or embarrassment.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The child **squirmed** in his seat all through the long sermon.
> - The question made her **squirm**, and she changed the subject.
> - He left them **squirming** under the weight of the silence.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and vivid, mildly negative — it captures the small, restless twisting of someone uncomfortable in body or conscience. Lighter than [writhe](writhe.md); the discomfort is awkwardness or unease rather than agony.
