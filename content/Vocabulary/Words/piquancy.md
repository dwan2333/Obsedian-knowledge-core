# piquancy

> [!definition] /ˈpiːkənsi/ · *noun*
> A pleasantly sharp, spicy quality of taste or smell. Figuratively, a stimulating, slightly provocative quality that makes something more interesting — *the gossip lent the evening some piquancy*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A squeeze of lime gave the dish a **piquancy** that the recipe badly needed.
> - The scandal added a certain **piquancy** to an otherwise dull season.
> - There was a **piquancy** to her wit — just enough sharpness to keep you alert.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and appreciative, always positive. It names a sharpness that *delights* rather than overwhelms — the welcome edge, not the excess. The adjective *piquant* is the more common form.
