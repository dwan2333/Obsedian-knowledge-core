# matrimonial

> [!definition] /ˌmætrɪˈmoʊniəl/ · *adjective*
> Of or relating to marriage or the relationship between a married couple.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The lawyer specialized in **matrimonial** disputes, from prenuptial agreements to divorce.
> - They signed the **matrimonial** contract before a notary and two witnesses.
> - Her **matrimonial** prospects improved once her father settled a generous dowry.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, neutral, and slightly legalistic; common in law, ceremony, and older prose. Reach for it over plain *marital* when the tone is official or documentary.
