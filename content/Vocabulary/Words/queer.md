# queer

> [!definition] /kwɪə/ · *adjective*
> Strange, odd, or peculiar; not as expected. (Also, informally, slightly unwell — *feeling queer*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **queer** feeling came over her, as if she had stood in that doorway before.
> - There was something **queer** about the house — too quiet, the clocks all stopped.
> - He went a bit **queer** after the fever and never quite recovered his old briskness.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> In this "strange/odd" sense, now dated and chiefly literary — common in older fiction, less so in modern speech. **Usage note:** today *queer* is principally used as a reclaimed term for LGBTQ+ identity, so the "odd" sense can read as archaic or, applied to people, be misread. Reach for "strange," "odd," or "uncanny" in contemporary writing unless the period flavour is wanted.
