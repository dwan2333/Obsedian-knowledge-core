# graceful

> [!definition] /ˈɡreɪsfʊl/ · *adjective*
> Showing grace in form, movement, or manner; elegant, smooth, and pleasing to watch. Figuratively, handling things with poise and tact.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The dancer's **graceful** leap seemed to hang in the air.
> - She made a **graceful** exit, thanking everyone without a trace of resentment.
> - The bridge's long, **graceful** curve was as much sculpture as engineering.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and warmly positive — it praises an ease and elegance that looks effortless, whether in a body, a gesture, or a shape. Note the figurative sense: to be *graceful* in defeat is to bear it with poise.
