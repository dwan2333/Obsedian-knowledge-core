# extramarital

> [!definition] /ˌɛkstrəˈmærɪtəl/ (ek-struh-MARR-ih-tul) · *adjective*
> (Of a sexual or romantic relationship) happening outside a person's marriage, with someone who is not their husband or wife. Most often in *extramarital affair*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The senator resigned after reports of an **extramarital** affair with a member of his staff.
> - The novel follows a doctor whose **extramarital** relationship slowly unravels his family.
> - Surveys on **extramarital** sex tend to undercount, since few people admit to it.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and clinical. It is the non-judging word of journalists, lawyers, and researchers, where everyday speech says *cheating* or *having an affair* and moral or religious language says *adultery*. It almost always comes before a noun: *extramarital affair, relationship, sex*. Mind the spelling: *marital* is "of marriage", while *martial* is "of war" (*martial law, martial arts*). From Latin *extra*, "outside", plus *marital*, from *maritus*, "husband".
