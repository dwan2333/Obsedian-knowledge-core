# gurney

> [!definition] /ˈɡɜːni/ · *noun*
> A wheeled stretcher or trolley used to move patients in a hospital.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - They wheeled him down the corridor on a **gurney**, the wheels squeaking.
> - A row of empty **gurneys** lined the wall outside the operating theatre.
> - She woke on a **gurney** under bright lights, unsure how she'd got there.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete, chiefly North American — the everyday hospital word for a wheeled stretcher (British English often says *trolley*). Carries an unavoidable clinical, emergency-room atmosphere.
