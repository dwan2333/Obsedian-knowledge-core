# vileness

> [!definition] /ˈvaɪlnəs/ · *noun*
> The quality of being vile — morally base, disgusting, or contemptible; foulness of character or act.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The letter's sheer **vileness** shocked even his enemies.
> - She had glimpsed a **vileness** in him that no charm could quite cover.
> - There is a special **vileness** in cruelty dressed up as kindness.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and severe — among the strong words for moral foulness, condemning both wicked acts and the baseness behind them. Close to [depravity](depravity.md), but with a sharper note of disgust. Use it for things that genuinely repel.
