# ominous

> [!definition] /ˈɒmɪnəs/ · *adjective*
> Giving the impression that something bad is about to happen; threatening, foreboding.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - An **ominous** silence fell over the room as the verdict was read.
> - **Ominous** grey clouds were piling up over the hills.
> - There was an **ominous** note in his voice that made her stop and listen.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish, literary, and negative — it points forward to coming trouble, a sign read as a warning. A favourite of suspenseful writing. Close to a [presentiment](presentiment.md) made visible in the world rather than felt within.
