# spiteful

> [!definition] /ˈspaɪtfʊl/ · *adjective*
> Wishing to hurt or annoy out of mean-spirited ill will; motivated by spite. (You met it as **spitefully**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - It was a small, **spiteful** act — tearing the page she most wanted to keep.
> - He spread the rumour out of pure **spite**, with nothing to gain by it.
> - She gave a **spiteful** little smile as the bad news was read out.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and strongly negative. *Spite* is petty, gratuitous malice — harm done for the pleasure of harming, not for any advantage. That pointlessness is what makes it uglier, in its small way, than calculated cruelty.
