# presentiment

> [!definition] /prɪˈzɛntɪmənt/ · *noun*
> A feeling that something is about to happen, especially something bad; a foreboding or premonition.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She left the house with a **presentiment** of disaster she could not shake.
> - He had a **presentiment**, the night before, that the letter would bring bad news.
> - Some dim **presentiment** made him turn back at the gate.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, neutral-to-ominous — it names an *intuitive dread* of the future, not a reasoned expectation. A favourite of suspenseful fiction, where it lets the narrator hint at coming catastrophe. Plainer cousins: "foreboding," "hunch."
