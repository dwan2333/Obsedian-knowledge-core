# penitent

> [!definition] /ˈpɛnɪtənt/ (PEN-ih-tunt) · *adjective (also noun)*
> Feeling or showing sorrow and regret for having done wrong. As a noun, a person who repents, especially one who confesses and seeks forgiveness in the Church.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He stood in the doorway with a **penitent** look and a bunch of flowers.
> - The company issued a **penitent** statement and promised to refund every customer.
> - The **penitents** walked barefoot to the shrine to ask forgiveness.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and somewhat literary, with a religious colouring; sympathetic, since it credits the person with real remorse. It is stronger and humbler than *sorry*. *Apologetic* is the everyday word, *contrite* a close synonym, and *remorseful* stresses the inner pain. The noun is *penitence*; *penance* is the act done to show it, and a *penitentiary* was first a place for it. The opposite is *unrepentant* or *impenitent*. From Latin *paenitere*, "to cause regret, to repent".
