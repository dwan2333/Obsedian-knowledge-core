# implicit

> [!definition] /ɪmˈplɪsɪt/ · *adjective*
> Implied or understood though not directly stated — *an implicit threat*. A second sense: absolute and unquestioning — *implicit trust, implicit obedience*.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - There was an **implicit** warning in his politeness that no one missed.
> - The agreement was never written down; it remained **implicit** between them.
> - She followed him with **implicit** faith, never once asking where they were going.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and precise, neutral in tone. The opposite of *explicit* (openly stated) in its first sense, and near *absolute* in its second — context separates them. A useful word for what a situation conveys without saying.
