# dud

> [!definition] /dʌd/ · *noun (also adjective)*
> A thing that fails to work or to meet expectations; a flop. As an adjective, faulty or useless (*a dud battery*). (Originally, a shell or firework that fails to go off.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The much-hyped sequel turned out to be a complete **dud**.
> - One of the fireworks was a **dud** and never left the ground.
> - He bought a cheap charger and, of course, it was a **dud**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and dismissive — a blunt, everyday word for something that disappoints by failing to perform. Light and a little contemptuous; covers gadgets, films, plans, and people who don't deliver.
