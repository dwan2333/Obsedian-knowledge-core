# tangible

> [!definition] /ˈtændʒɪbəl/ · *adjective*
> Perceptible by touch; concrete and real rather than abstract — able to be clearly grasped or measured.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Months of talks produced no **tangible** results.
> - There was a **tangible** tension in the room you could almost touch.
> - They wanted **tangible** proof, not promises.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral-to-positive — it values what is solid, real, and demonstrable over the vague or merely promised. Often figurative (*tangible benefits, a tangible atmosphere*). The opposite is *intangible* or *abstract*.
