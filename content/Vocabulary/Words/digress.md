# digress

> [!definition] /daɪˈɡrɛs/ · *verb*
> To leave the main subject temporarily in speech or writing; to wander off the point.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The lecturer **digressed** into a long story about his student days.
> - "But I **digress**," she said, returning to the matter at hand.
> - The memoir **digresses** so often that the thread is hard to follow.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral — a precise term for straying from the topic, common in writing and self-aware speech (*to digress*). Mildly self-deprecating when used of oneself; the noun is *digression*.
