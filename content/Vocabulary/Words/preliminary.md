# preliminary

> [!definition] /prɪˈlɪmɪnəri/ · *adjective (also noun)*
> Coming before and preparing for the main event or business; introductory. As a noun, a preliminary action or stage.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - After the usual **preliminary** courtesies, they got down to the real negotiation.
> - The **preliminary** results were promising, but the full study was months away.
> - He skipped the **preliminaries** and asked her outright.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, common in legal, academic, and business writing (*preliminary hearing, preliminary findings*). It marks something as provisional and preparatory — not yet the real thing, but clearing the way for it.
