# antsy

> [!definition] /ˈæntsi/ · *adjective*
> Restless, fidgety, and impatient; unable to keep still or settle, often from boredom or nervousness. (Informal, chiefly North American — as if one had "ants in one's pants.")

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The kids got **antsy** halfway through the long car journey.
> - Investors grew **antsy** as the announcement was delayed again.
> - I get **antsy** if I sit at a desk too long.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal and light — a colloquial word for jittery restlessness, less serious than "anxious" and more physical than "impatient." Best in casual writing and speech; it would feel out of place in a formal report.
