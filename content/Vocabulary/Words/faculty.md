# faculty

> [!definition] /ˈfækəlti/ · *noun*
> An inherent power of the mind or body — *the faculty of reason, of speech, of memory*. (Other senses: the teaching staff of a university, or one of its main divisions; an authorization to act.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Age had dimmed his sight but left his **faculties** of reason perfectly sharp.
> - The blow robbed her, briefly, of the **faculty** of speech.
> - He possessed a rare **faculty** for putting strangers instantly at ease.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral. In the "mental power" sense it is literary and a touch clinical; reach for it to name a *specific capacity* of the mind rather than general cleverness. (In a university context the same word means the staff or a department — context keeps the senses apart.)
