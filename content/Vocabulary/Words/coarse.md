# coarse

> [!definition] /kɔːrs/ · *adjective*
> Rough or harsh in texture, made of large or uneven particles — *coarse sand*, *coarse cloth*. Of a person or manner, crude and vulgar: lacking refinement in speech, taste, or behaviour.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The blanket was woven from a **coarse**, scratchy wool.
> - He told a **coarse** joke that silenced the dinner table.
> - Beneath the fine clothes was a **coarse**, ill-mannered man.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday; neutral for textures, distinctly disapproving for people. Of manner it is blunter and earthier than *crude* or *vulgar* and carries a whiff of class judgement. Note its near-homophone *course* — wholly unrelated.
