# cassock

> [!definition] /ˈkæsək/ · *noun*
> A long, close-fitting robe, usually black, worn by clergy and church officials.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The young priest's **cassock** was worn shiny at the elbows from years of wear.
> - He gathered up the skirts of his **cassock** and hurried across the muddy courtyard.
> - A **cassock** hung on the door, the only sign of his calling in an otherwise bare room.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and specific — the exact word for the garment. Useful precision in religious or period writing; there is no plainer everyday synonym.
