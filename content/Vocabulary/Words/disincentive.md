# disincentive

> [!definition] /ˌdɪsɪnˈsɛntɪv/ · *noun*
> Something that discourages a particular action or effort; a deterrent, especially an economic one.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - High taxes on overtime act as a **disincentive** to working longer hours.
> - The paperwork is a real **disincentive** to applying at all.
> - They worried the fine was too small to be any **disincentive**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and neutral, common in economics, policy, and management writing — it names a factor that makes an action less attractive, the mirror image of an *incentive*. Dry and analytical in tone.
