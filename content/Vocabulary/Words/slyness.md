# slyness

> [!definition] /ˈslaɪnəs/ · *noun*
> Secretive cunning; the quality of being clever in a furtive, underhand way.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - There was a **slyness** in his sideways glance that put her on her guard.
> - The cat watched the birds with a patient **slyness**, perfectly still.
> - He answered with a **slyness** that gave away nothing and hinted at everything.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Mildly informal and disapproving, though lighter than outright deceit — *slyness* can be playful or sinister depending on context. It always carries the sense of something *hidden*: cleverness that doesn't show its hand.
