# writhe

> [!definition] /raɪð/ · *verb*
> To twist and squirm the body, especially in pain; to contort. Figuratively, to suffer acute embarrassment or distress. (You met it as **writhing**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He lay **writhing** on the ground, clutching his ankle.
> - The snake **writhed** out of his grasp and was gone.
> - She **writhed** inwardly at the memory of what she'd said.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday-to-literary and vivid, negative — it pictures the body twisting under pain or the mind squirming under shame. Stronger and more anguished than "wriggle"; reach for it when the discomfort is acute.
