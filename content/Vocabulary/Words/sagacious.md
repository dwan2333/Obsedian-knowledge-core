# sagacious

> [!definition] /səˈɡeɪʃəs/ · *adjective*
> Having keen judgment and discernment; wise in a shrewd, practical way.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A **sagacious** old lawyer, she saw the trap in the contract that everyone else had missed.
> - His **sagacious** investments, made quietly over decades, left the family comfortable for life.
> - The **sagacious** reader will notice what the narrator is careful not to say.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and admiring. It praises wisdom joined to *practical sharpness* — warmer and weightier than [shrewd](shrewd.md), grander than "clever." In plain speech, "wise" or "astute" carries the sense without the elevation.
