# perpetual

> [!definition] /pəˈpɛtʃuəl/ · *adjective*
> Never ending or changing; continuing endlessly. Loosely, occurring so often it seems constant (*her perpetual complaints*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The far north has weeks of **perpetual** daylight in summer.
> - He lived in **perpetual** fear of being found out.
> - I'm tired of your **perpetual** excuses.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral in the strict sense (everlasting), but often mildly exasperated in the loose sense (*perpetual* interruptions). Grander than "constant"; reach for it for things that genuinely never cease — or, with a sigh, for things that feel that way.
