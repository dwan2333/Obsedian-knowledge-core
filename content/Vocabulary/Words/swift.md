# swift

> [!definition] /swɪft/ · *adjective*
> Moving or happening with great speed; quick and prompt. (As a noun, a fast-flying bird.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She gave a **swift** glance over her shoulder and slipped through the gate.
> - The response was **swift** and merciless: he was dismissed by morning.
> - With one **swift** stroke he cut the rope.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral, a touch more literary and vivid than "fast" — it stresses smooth, sudden quickness, often of an action or a response. Pairs naturally with movement, decisions, and retribution.
