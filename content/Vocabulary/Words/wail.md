# wail

> [!definition] /weɪl/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To cry out in a long, loud, high voice in grief or pain; to lament. As a noun, such a cry. (You met it as **wailed**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The mourners **wailed** aloud as the coffin was lowered.
> - "It isn't fair!" she **wailed**.
> - A **wail** of sirens rose from the street below.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday-to-literary and vivid — it captures grief or distress given full, unrestrained voice, louder and more anguished than weeping. Also used of sirens and wind. Can be faintly mocking when applied to childish complaint.
