# apportion

> [!definition] /əˈpɔːrʃən/ · *verb*
> To divide something up and share it out, giving each party a fitting or proportional amount — *apportion the funds, apportion the blame*. (You met it as **apportioned**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The court will **apportion** liability between the two drivers according to fault.
> - Profits were **apportioned** among the partners in proportion to what each had invested.
> - It's not for me to **apportion** blame; I only want to fix the problem.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, neutral, and common in legal, financial, and administrative writing. It carries a sense of *fair or proportional* division — more deliberate than a casual "split up" or "share out."
