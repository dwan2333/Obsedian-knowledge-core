# competent

> [!definition] /ˈkɒmpɪtənt/ · *adjective*
> Having enough skill or ability to do something well enough; capable, though not necessarily brilliant.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She's a thoroughly **competent** driver, if not an adventurous one.
> - The repair was **competent** but uninspired.
> - You must be deemed **competent** to stand trial before the case proceeds.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral-to-faintly-faint — "competent" is genuine praise for adequacy, but it quietly stops short of excellence (calling a performance "competent" can damn with mild praise). Note the legal sense: having the capacity to act or testify.
