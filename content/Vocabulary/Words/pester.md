# pester

> [!definition] /ˈpɛstə/ · *verb*
> To trouble someone with persistent, irritating requests or attention. (You met it as **pestered**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The children **pestered** him for sweets until he gave in just to have peace.
> - She **pestered** the office with calls every day until the matter was settled.
> - Stop **pestering** your sister and let her read.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and mildly negative — the plain, common word for wearing someone down with repeated nagging. The everyday cousin of formal [importunity](importunity.md): same dogged pressure, lower register.
