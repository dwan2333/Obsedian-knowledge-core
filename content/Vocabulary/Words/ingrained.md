# ingrained

> [!definition] /ɪnˈɡreɪnd/ · *adjective*
> (Of a habit, attitude, or belief) so firmly fixed by long existence that it is hard to change; deeply embedded. (Also, of dirt, worked deep into a surface.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The prejudice was so **ingrained** that he no longer noticed he held it.
> - Years of training left an **ingrained** habit of checking the locks twice.
> - No amount of scrubbing shifted the **ingrained** grime.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral-to-negative — it stresses how deep and hard-to-remove something is, usually a fault, bias, or habit. The figurative sense is the common one; reach for it when a trait has become second nature, for better or (usually) worse.
