# martyr

> [!definition] /ˈmɑːtə/ · *noun*
> A person who is killed or made to suffer greatly for their religious or other beliefs. (Loosely and often sarcastically, one who makes a great show of their suffering — *playing the martyr*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The early Christians honoured their **martyrs** above all the saints.
> - She became a **martyr** to the cause, imprisoned and finally exiled for it.
> - "Stop playing the **martyr**," he said; "no one asked you to do all the washing-up."

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and weighty in the literal sense — reverent toward genuine sacrifice. The loose sense is dismissive, mocking someone who advertises their suffering for sympathy. Tone and context tell the two sharply apart.
