# ecstatic

> [!definition] /ɪkˈstætɪk/ · *adjective*
> Feeling or showing overwhelming happiness or rapturous delight; carried away by intense emotion. (In its older sense, involving religious ecstasy or trance.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She was **ecstatic** when the letter of acceptance finally arrived.
> - The crowd gave an **ecstatic** roar as the winning goal went in.
> - He described the vision in **ecstatic**, almost feverish terms, as if still half in a trance.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday-to-literary and strongly positive — joy at its most intense, far beyond "pleased" or "glad." In its older religious sense it borders on the rapt trance of the mystic; in daily use it is sheer exuberant delight.
