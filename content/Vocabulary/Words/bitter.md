# bitter

> [!definition] /ˈbɪtə/ · *adjective*
> Full of resentment and lingering hurt — *bitter about the betrayal*. Also: sharp and unpleasant to the taste; piercingly cold (*a bitter wind*). (You met it as **bitterly**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Years later he was still **bitter** about the promotion that went to a lesser man.
> - She wept **bitterly**, and would not be comforted.
> - The coffee was **bitter**, and the wind outside was **bitter** too.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and versatile. The emotional sense — resentment that has curdled and won't let go — is the most charged; it pairs with grief, defeat, and old wrongs. The taste and cold senses are plainer and physical. *Bitterly* intensifies weeping, complaining, and regretting.
