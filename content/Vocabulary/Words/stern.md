# stern

> [!definition] /stɜːn/ · *adjective*
> Serious, strict, and unrelenting, especially in discipline or disapproval; forbidding in manner. (As a noun, the rear of a ship.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The headmaster's **stern** look was enough to silence the class.
> - She is a **stern** judge of her own work, and rarely satisfied.
> - He issued a **stern** warning against any repeat of the behaviour.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral-to-negative — it conveys severity and firmness, sometimes admirably principled, sometimes coldly forbidding. Pairs with looks, warnings, judges, and discipline. Note the unrelated nautical noun (a ship's stern).
