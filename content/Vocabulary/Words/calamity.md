# calamity

> [!definition] /kəˈlæmɪti/ · *noun*
> An event causing great harm, loss, or distress; a disaster. (You met it as **calamities**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The failure of the harvest was a **calamity** for the whole valley.
> - One **calamity** followed another until the family had nothing left.
> - He has a gift for turning a small mishap into a full **calamity**.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and strong, negative — a grave word for serious disaster, weightier than "mishap" or "trouble." Can be used half-jokingly to inflate a minor misfortune for comic effect, but its core sense is genuine catastrophe.
