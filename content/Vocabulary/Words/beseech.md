# beseech

> [!definition] /bɪˈsiːtʃ/ · *verb*
> To beg or implore someone earnestly and anxiously. (You met it as **besought**, the older past tense; *beseeched* is also used.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She **besought** him, with tears, not to go.
> - "Hear me," he **beseeched** the council, "before you decide."
> - They **besought** the king for mercy on the prisoners.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and archaic-flavoured — far more urgent and emotional than "ask," weighted with desperation. At home in scripture, old fiction, and high drama; in plain speech, "beg" or "plead" carries the sense.
