# conveyance

> [!definition] /kənˈveɪəns/ · *noun*
> (1) A means of transport — a vehicle. (2) The act of carrying or transporting. (3) In law, the transfer of property from one owner to another, or the document that does it.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - With no car and no bus, a borrowed bicycle was their only **conveyance**.
> - The **conveyance** of goods across the border was taxed at every stage.
> - The solicitor handled the **conveyance** of the house, and the deeds changed hands on Friday.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and somewhat old-fashioned in the "vehicle" sense (charmingly so); precise and current in the legal sense. Neutral throughout. In everyday speech "transport" or "vehicle" is plainer; *conveyance* suits formal or period writing.
