# obscure

> [!definition] /əbˈskjʊər/ · *adjective, also verb*
> *adj.* Not clearly expressed or hard to understand; also, little-known or unimportant. *verb* To hide from view or make unclear.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The poem's meaning stayed **obscure** even after a third reading.
> - He was an **obscure** clerk until the scandal made his name famous overnight.
> - Thick fog began to **obscure** the harbour lights one by one.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral to mildly negative; works in everyday and literary contexts. As a verb it is more vivid than *hide*; as an adjective, sharper than *unclear* or *unknown*.
