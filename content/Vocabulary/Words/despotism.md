# despotism

> [!definition] /ˈdɛspətɪzəm/ · *noun*
> Rule by a despot — the exercise of absolute power by one person or body, especially in a cruel and oppressive way; tyranny.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The revolution promised to replace centuries of **despotism** with the rule of law.
> - Unchecked, even a well-meaning leader can slide into **despotism**.
> - The pamphlet denounced the governor for his petty **despotism** over every village.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, at home in political and historical writing, and firmly negative. It names not just power but *unaccountable, oppressive* power. The contrasting ideal is [emancipation](emancipation.md) — the breaking of such control.
