# scrupulous

> [!definition] /ˈskruːpjələs/ · *adjective*
> Diligently thorough and exact in attending to detail. Also: carefully principled — taking pains to do what is right and to avoid even small wrongs.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The auditor was **scrupulous**, reconciling every figure to the last cent.
> - She was too **scrupulous** to keep the change the cashier had given her by mistake.
> - His notes were kept with **scrupulous** care, each source dated and cross-referenced.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, strongly positive. It blends two senses — painstaking accuracy and moral exactness — so it praises both competence and integrity. Stronger and more admiring than the plainer *meticulous*, *conscientious*, or *painstaking*, which point only to care, not conscience.
