# opine

> [!definition] /əˈpaɪn/ · *verb*
> To state as one's opinion; to express a view, often with a touch of formality or self-importance. (You met it as **opined**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - "The whole scheme is doomed," he **opined**, and returned to his newspaper.
> - The critics **opined** at length on a film almost none of them had finished.
> - She **opined** that the weather would turn, and, as usual, she was right.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and often faintly ironic — writers reach for *opined* to gently mark a pronouncement as more confident than it deserves. In plain speech "said" or "remarked" is neutral; *opine* adds a whiff of the armchair authority.
