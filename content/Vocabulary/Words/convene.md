# convene

> [!definition] /kənˈviːn/ (kun-VEEN) · *verb*
> To call people together for a formal meeting, or (of a group) to come together for one. (You met it as **convened**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The prime minister **convened** an emergency meeting of the cabinet at midnight.
> - The committee will **convene** again on Monday to vote on the report.
> - A panel of experts was **convened** to investigate the cause of the crash.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal; the word of parliaments, courts, boards, and committees (*convene a meeting, a session, a panel, a summit, a grand jury*). It works both ways: someone convenes a group, or the group convenes. Plainer options are *call a meeting*, *meet*, and *gather*; the opposite is *adjourn* (to close a meeting for now). From Latin *convenire*, "to come together" (*con-* "together" + *venire* "to come"), the root of *convention*, *convenient*, and *venue*.
