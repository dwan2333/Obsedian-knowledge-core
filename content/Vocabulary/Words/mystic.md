# mystic

> [!definition] /ˈmɪstɪk/ · *noun (also adjective)*
> A person who seeks direct union with the divine through contemplation, prayer, or ecstatic experience. As an adjective, spiritually symbolic or beyond ordinary understanding.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The old monk was revered as a **mystic**, said to fall into trances that lasted for days.
> - Her poetry has a **mystic** strain, forever reaching past the visible world.
> - To the **mystic**, the rituals mattered less than the silent moment of union they prepared for.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and reverent-to-neutral; carries an air of the otherworldly. Distinct from *fanatic* (which is about zeal, not insight) — a mystic seeks inward communion, not a cause to fight for.
