# wrangle

> [!definition] /ˈræŋɡəl/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To argue or dispute noisily and at length, often over something petty. As a noun, such a dispute. (You met it as **wrangling**. In US usage, also to herd livestock.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The committee **wrangled** for hours over a single line of the budget.
> - After months of legal **wrangling**, the estate was finally settled.
> - They were still **wrangling** about whose turn it was to pay.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and mildly negative — it frames an argument as drawn-out, undignified, and a touch tiresome. Less heated than a full quarrel, more persistent than a disagreement.
