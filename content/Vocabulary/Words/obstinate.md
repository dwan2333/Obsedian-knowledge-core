# obstinate

> [!definition] /ˈɒbstɪnət/ · *adjective*
> Stubbornly refusing to change one's mind, course, or behaviour despite good reason to. Also of things — *an obstinate stain*, *an obstinate cough* — that resist every effort to remove them.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He was too **obstinate** to admit he had taken a wrong turn.
> - The negotiations stalled against her **obstinate** refusal to compromise.
> - No amount of scrubbing shifted the **obstinate** mark on the carpet.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and mildly disapproving — it frames the firmness as unreasonable, where *determined* or *steadfast* would praise it. Reach for it when persistence has hardened into a fault.
