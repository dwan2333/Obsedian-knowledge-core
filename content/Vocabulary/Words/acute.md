# acute

> [!definition] /əˈkjuːt/ · *adjective*
> Sharp and intense — of pain or a crisis (*acute distress*); keen and perceptive — of a mind or sense (*an acute observer*); (of an illness) severe and short, as opposed to chronic. (You met it as **acutest**, the superlative.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The shortage of nurses has become **acute**.
> - She has an **acute** ear for falseness in a voice.
> - He felt an **acute** embarrassment that brought colour to his face.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and neutral, ranging across senses that context separates: intense (pain, crisis), keen (perception), and medically severe-but-brief. In the "perceptive" sense it praises sharpness of mind, close to [shrewd](shrewd.md).
