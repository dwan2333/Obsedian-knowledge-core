# aquiline

> [!definition] /ˈækwɪlaɪn/ · *adjective*
> (Especially of a nose) curved like an eagle's beak; hooked and prominent. More broadly, eagle-like. (From Latin *aquila*, eagle.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - His **aquiline** nose and dark brows gave him a fierce, hawkish look.
> - She recognised the **aquiline** profile from the old family portraits at once.
> - There was something **aquiline** in his gaze, sharp and unblinking.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, neutral-to-flattering — an *aquiline* nose suggests nobility, sharpness, and a certain hauteur. A staple of character description in classic fiction; in plain speech "hooked nose" is the blunt equivalent, without the dignity.
