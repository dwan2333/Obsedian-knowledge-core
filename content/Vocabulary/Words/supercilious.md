# supercilious

> [!definition] /ˌsuːpəˈsɪliəs/ · *adjective*
> Behaving as though one is superior to others; haughtily disdainful and contemptuous.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **supercilious** waiter made it clear they could not afford the place.
> - She gave a **supercilious** little smile and turned away.
> - His **supercilious** tone, as if explaining the obvious to a child, infuriated them.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and negative — it captures cold, looking-down-the-nose superiority, the disdain of someone who thinks others beneath them. Where [insolent](insolent.md) is rudely defiant, *supercilious* is loftily contemptuous, often without a word out of place.
