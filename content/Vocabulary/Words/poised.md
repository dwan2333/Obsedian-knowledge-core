# poised

> [!definition] /pɔɪzd/ · *adjective*
> Calm, self-assured, and in control of oneself, especially under pressure; graceful in the way one stands and moves. Also, with *to* or *for* — ready and in position, on the point of doing something (*poised to win*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She stayed **poised** through an hour of hostile questions and never raised her voice.
> - The company is **poised** to overtake its largest rival by the end of the year.
> - The diver stood **poised** on the edge of the board, arms raised.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral to slightly formal, and a compliment when said of a person. *Calm* describes how someone feels; *poised* describes how they carry themselves, so it suits interviews, performances, and public moments. *Poised to* is common in news and business writing for something about to happen, and it works for bad outcomes as well as good ones (*poised to collapse*). The noun is *poise*.
