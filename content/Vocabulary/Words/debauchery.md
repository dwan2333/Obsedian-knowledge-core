# debauchery

> [!definition] /dɪˈbɔːtʃəri/ · *noun*
> Excessive indulgence in sensual pleasures — drinking, feasting, and sexual licence pursued without restraint. Often used of a whole way of life given over to such excess.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The young heir squandered the estate in a few short years of gambling and **debauchery**.
> - Behind the elegant façade, the court was a scene of nightly **debauchery**.
> - He looked back on the **debauchery** of his student days with a mixture of nostalgia and shame.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, strongly negative — it carries a note of moral judgement that plainer words like *partying* lack. Reach for it when you want the weight of ruin and dissolution, not mere fun.
