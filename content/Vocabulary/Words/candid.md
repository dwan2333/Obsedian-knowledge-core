# candid

> [!definition] /ˈkændɪd/ · *adjective*
> Frank and honest; speaking openly, even when the truth is awkward. (Also of a photograph, unposed.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - To be **candid**, I never thought the plan would work in the first place.
> - She gave a **candid** account of her failures that won the room's respect.
> - The most revealing shots were the **candid** ones, taken before anyone had posed.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral-to-positive — *candour* is usually a virtue, the willingness to be honest at some cost to comfort. Warmer and more principled than "blunt"; a candid person is truthful, not merely tactless. The opposite of the guarded or [implicit](implicit.md).
