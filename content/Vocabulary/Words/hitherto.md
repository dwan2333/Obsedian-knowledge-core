# hitherto

> [!definition] /ˌhɪðərˈtuː/ · *adverb*
> Up to this point in time; until now.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The discovery revealed a species **hitherto** unknown to science.
> - **Hitherto** he had spoken little, but now the words poured out.
> - The treaty opened markets **hitherto** closed to foreign trade.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, literary, and faintly archaic. Lends weight and a sense of turning point; in plain speech you would simply say *until now* or *so far*. Synonyms *heretofore* and *thus far* share its bookish tone.
