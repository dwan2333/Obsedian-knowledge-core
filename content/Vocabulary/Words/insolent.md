# insolent

> [!definition] /ˈɪnsələnt/ · *adjective*
> Rudely disrespectful; openly contemptuous of those one should defer to.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **insolent** clerk looked him up and down and went back to his newspaper.
> - She gave an **insolent** shrug that said the rules did not apply to her.
> - His **insolent** reply to the magistrate cost him a night in the cells.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal-ish and strongly negative. *Insolent* is rudeness aimed *upward* — at authority, elders, or anyone owed respect — and it adds a sneer of contempt that plain "rude" lacks.
