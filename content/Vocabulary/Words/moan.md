# moan

> [!definition] /məʊn/ · *noun (also verb)*
> A long, low sound of pain, grief, or pleasure. As a verb, to make such a sound — or, informally, to complain and grumble.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - A low **moan** came from the next room, and she ran to the door.
> - The wind gave a continual **moan** through the gaps in the old shutters.
> - He **moans** about the cold, the food, the hours — about everything, in fact.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral in the "sound of suffering" sense; informal and mildly disapproving in the "complain" sense (a British colloquial favourite). Tone and context separate a moan of real pain from a moan about the weather.
