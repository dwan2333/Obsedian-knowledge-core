# wunderkind

> [!definition] /ˈvʊndəkɪnt/ · /ˈwʌndəkɪnd/ · *noun*
> A person who achieves great success at a young age; a prodigy or whiz-kid. (A German loanword, literally "wonder-child.")

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The thirty-year-old **wunderkind** had already founded and sold two companies.
> - Once the **wunderkind** of the violin world, she now teaches quietly in the country.
> - Every newsroom has its **wunderkind**, and its quiet veterans who do the real work.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Informal-to-journalistic, usually admiring but sometimes faintly sceptical — the word can carry a hint that the brilliance is young, hyped, or not yet tested by time. Keeps its German plural *Wunderkinder*, though *wunderkinds* is also used.
