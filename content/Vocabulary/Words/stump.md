# stump

> [!definition] /stʌmp/ · *noun*
> The short part of something left after the rest has been cut, broken, or worn away — the base of a felled tree, the remnant of a lost limb, the end of a spent candle. (You met it as **stumps**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The old man's leg ended in a **stump** he had learned, over years, to walk on.
> - Only blackened **stumps** remained where the orchard had stood.
> - She wrote with a **stump** of pencil worn down almost to nothing.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and concrete, neutral. Note the idioms it spawns: *to be stumped* (baffled), and *on the stump* (campaigning, from speaking atop a tree stump). Here the core sense is the literal remnant — what is left when most is gone.
