# latter

> [!definition] /ˈlætər/ · *adjective / noun*
> The second-mentioned of two things; also, later or nearer the end of a period.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - Tea or coffee was offered, and she chose the **latter**.
> - In his **latter** years he wrote almost nothing.
> - Of the two routes, the former is faster but the **latter** is far prettier.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and slightly formal; common in writing where precision matters. Avoid it when readers must hunt back to recall which of two things you mean.

## Related words
