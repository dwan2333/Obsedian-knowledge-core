# effrontery

> [!definition] /ɪˈfrʌntəri/ · *noun*
> Shameless, insolent boldness; barefaced audacity in doing or saying something improper.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He had the **effrontery** to ask for a reference after being dismissed for theft.
> - She stared back with such **effrontery** that the accusation died on his lips.
> - It took real **effrontery** to charge those prices and call it a bargain.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal and literary, sharply disapproving — yet often laced with reluctant astonishment at the sheer nerve. Use it for boldness that *should* have been checked by shame and wasn't. Close cousins: *gall, nerve, cheek*.
