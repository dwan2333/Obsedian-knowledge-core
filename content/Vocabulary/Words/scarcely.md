# scarcely

> [!definition] /ˈskɛəsli/ · *adverb*
> Only just; barely; almost not at all. Also used to mean "surely not" (*you can scarcely expect me to agree*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She had **scarcely** sat down when the bell rang again.
> - There was **scarcely** enough bread to go round.
> - He could **scarcely** be blamed for a mistake no one had warned him about.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral, slightly formal — a touch more literary than "hardly," with which it is otherwise interchangeable. Note that it carries a negative force, so it doesn't take another negative (*scarcely anyone*, not *scarcely no one*).
