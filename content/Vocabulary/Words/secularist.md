# secularist

> [!definition] /ˈsɛkjʊlərɪst/ · *noun*
> A person who believes that religion should be kept separate from public, civic, and political life. (Related: *secular*, worldly or non-religious; *secularism*, the principle itself.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - As a committed **secularist**, he argued that the schools should teach no catechism at all.
> - The **secularists** and the clergy clashed over whose authority the new law would answer to.
> - She was no atheist, but a **secularist** who simply wanted the Church out of the courtroom.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Formal, at home in political, historical, and religious debate; neutral in itself, though charged depending on the speaker. Names a *stance on the place of religion*, not a disbelief in God — that distinction is the whole point of the word.
