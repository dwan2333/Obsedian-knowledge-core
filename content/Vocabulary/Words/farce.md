# farce

> [!definition] /fɑːs/ · *noun*
> An absurd, ridiculous, and badly mismanaged situation; a travesty. (Originally and still, a broad comic play built on improbable, exaggerated events.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The election descended into **farce**, with ballots lost and counts redone twice.
> - The trial was a **farce** — the verdict had clearly been decided in advance.
> - They staged a French bedroom **farce**, all slamming doors and mistaken identities.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and contemptuous in the "ridiculous mess" sense — it dismisses an event as so chaotic or rigged as to be a mockery of what it claimed to be. Neutral-to-technical in the theatrical sense. Kin to the [ludicrous](ludicrous.md).
