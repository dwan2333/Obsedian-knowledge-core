# upend

> [!definition] /ʌpˈɛnd/ · *verb*
> To set or turn something on its end or upside down. Figuratively, to overturn or disrupt drastically (*to upend expectations*).

> [!example] In a sentence
> - He **upended** the crate to use it as a stool.
> - The new evidence **upended** the prosecution's whole case.
> - Cheap streaming **upended** the music industry within a decade.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and neutral — literal in the "turn on end" sense, vivid and increasingly popular in the figurative "disrupt" sense (a favourite of business and tech writing). Stronger and more sudden than "change"; it implies a complete overturning.
