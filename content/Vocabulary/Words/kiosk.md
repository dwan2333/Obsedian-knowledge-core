# kiosk

> [!definition] /ˈkiːɒsk/ · *noun*
> A small open-fronted booth or stall, used for selling newspapers, snacks, or tickets, or for a self-service screen.

> [!example] In a sentence
> - She bought a paper from the **kiosk** on the corner.
> - Check in at the self-service **kiosk** before joining the queue.
> - A coffee **kiosk** had appeared overnight in the station concourse.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral and concrete — the standard word for a small stall or booth, increasingly for the touchscreen self-service kind. From Turkish via French; carries no particular colour beyond the everyday street or terminal.
