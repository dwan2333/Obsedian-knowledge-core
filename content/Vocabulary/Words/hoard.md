# hoard

> [!definition] /hɔːd/ · *verb (also noun)*
> To amass and hide away a store of money, food, or goods, often more than one needs. As a noun, the hidden store itself. (You met it as **hoarding**.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The old man **hoarded** every coin, living in rags atop a fortune.
> - Squirrels **hoard** nuts against the winter.
> - When rationing was announced, people began **hoarding** flour and salt.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Neutral for animals and prudent storing; disapproving for people, where it implies miserly or fearful over-accumulation. Don't confuse it with *horde* (a crowd). Pairs with money, treasure, supplies — and with anxiety.
