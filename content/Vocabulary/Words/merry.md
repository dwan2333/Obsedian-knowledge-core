# merry

> [!definition] /ˈmɛri/ · *adjective*
> Cheerful and full of high-spirited gaiety; festive. (Informally, also mildly drunk — *a bit merry*.)

> [!example] In a sentence
> - The **merry** party spilled out of the tavern singing into the snow.
> - She was in a **merry** mood, laughing at everything and nothing.
> - After two glasses he grew **merry** and started telling stories.

> [!info] Register & connotation
> Everyday and warm, positive — though a touch old-fashioned and festive in flavour (Christmas, taverns, song). Lighter and more sociable than "happy"; merriness is cheer that bubbles outward. The opposite of [morose](morose.md).
