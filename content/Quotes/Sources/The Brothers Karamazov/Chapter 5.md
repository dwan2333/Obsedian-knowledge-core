# The Brothers Karamazov — Chapter 5

*Lines kept from **Chapter 5**. Part of [The Brothers Karamazov](<The Brothers Karamazov (Main).md>).*

---

> [!quote] The realist disbelieves the miracle (p. 53)
> "The genuine realist, if he is an unbeliever, will always find strength and ability to disbelieve in the miraculous, and if he is confronted with a miracle as an irrefutable fact he would rather disbelieve his own senses than admit the fact."

> [!quote] Faith comes before the miracle (p. 53)
> "Faith does not, in the realist, spring from the miracle but the miracle from faith."

> [!quote] A youth of our last epoch (p. 54)
> "Add to that, he was to some extent a youth of our last [epoch](<../../../Vocabulary/Words/epoch.md>) — that is, honest in nature, desiring the truth, seeking for it and believing in it, and seeking to serve it at once with all the strength of his soul, seeking for immediate action, and ready to sacrifice everything, life itself, for it."

> [!quote] Socialism as the atheistic question (p. 54)
> "For socialism is not merely the labor question, it is before all things the atheistic question, the question of the form taken by atheism today, the question of the tower of Babel built without God, not to mount to heaven from earth but to set up heaven on earth."

> [!quote] A two-edged weapon — freedom or Satanic pride (p. 59)
> "It is true, perhaps, that this instrument which had stood the test of a thousand years for the moral regeneration of a man from slavery to freedom and to moral perfectibility may be a two-edged weapon and it may lead some not to humility and complete self-control but to the most Satanic pride, that is, to bondage and not to freedom."
