I haven't been writing the journal for the past four days. I will provided an brief account of the events that I find intriguing.  On Saturday, I recalled my dad spending a day with me and my brother.  While having lunch together, he said to us that him and the mistress are not getting back together, the reason being her ludicrous buffooneries and her rapaciousness. He said firmly that they are getting a divorce and they are wrangling in regard  to the sum of money she wanted .  Withing each day, there were some new caprices between them, and for me and my brother, we both think it would best for them to get divorced.  We were merely tired of their matrimonial disputes. 

Another calamity happened between Saturday night and Sunday. I had another quarrel with my girl friend on Saturday night because she was trying to talk to me and I was busy watching the world cup match for between Japan and Tunisia.  Shortly after the match begin where she could no longer withstand my ignorance towards her, my girl friend hung up the phone and I did not bother to call her back because I was too captivated by the game.
