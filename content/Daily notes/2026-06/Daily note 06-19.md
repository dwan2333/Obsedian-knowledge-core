With regard to the farce, my father brought my half sister back to our home and he is back together again with his mistress. Me and her lived at different places but not too far apart. Within ten minute drive we could reach each other's doorway. What baffled me is why ? What is the purpose of bringing the daughter over here. Is not that I do not want to see her, or she is not welcomed, but at this state where my dad and her were fighting over their matrimonial matters, her mother wanted her to be here.  The step mom obviously had an incentive for passing her over here instead of an mere caprice.  Is not that I am being cynical, but what could you expect from someone who manipulate their daughter out of self interest, who put an ludicrous act to our door step with grievances about our father, who act as an mistress while my mother was debilitated with cancer. There is no way you would convince me that she wanted her daughter to be her out of an whimsical touch. So I though about this thoroughly. What is she really planning? The night of her frantic scrambles, she kept mentioning that she want a divorce, and how much she hated my dad.  She wanted me to know these dissipations of my father so that I would despise him ? or merely because how desperate she is ? A mixture of both perhaps ?
